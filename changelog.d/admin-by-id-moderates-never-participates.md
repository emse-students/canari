### Fixed - by id, whoever moderates a post reads it to moderate, and participates only where an ordinary reader could

The moderation read of a post, its comments and its replay follows the one moderation predicate - a platform admin, a BDE moderator within its spaces (owner, 2026-11-15: "Oui, dans ses espaces"), a post scheduled later excepted for the latter until it is published or reported ("Exclure les posts programmes") - and is logged; reacting, commenting, voting, republishing, reporting and being mentioned ask every moderator the ordinary reader's right, and where they may not act a card draws no reaction and shows its comments read-only. [The moderation read](docs/wiki/moderation-and-blocking.md#the-moderation-read-of-a-post-its-comments-and-its-replay-owner-2026-11-15).
