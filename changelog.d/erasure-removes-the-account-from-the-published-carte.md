### Fixed - an erased account leaves the published carte de la vie asso

An erased person's member card, roster entry and printed directory name stayed on the public map
until its next publication; the erasure now removes them (a line published before rosters were
stored is withheld until the map is republished), and a stale editor can no longer publish or save
a non-member back ([account-erasure](docs/wiki/account-erasure.md#the-dispositions-and-why)).
