### Changed - the iPhone deletes its former seed mirror instead of folding it

With the `1.7.0` floor the fold of an older build's `graine_seeds.json` is replaced by a delete under the mirror lock; the App Group holds the one mirror ([legacy-compatibility](docs/wiki/legacy-compatibility.md#entries-170-retires-the-floor-raise-of-both-estates-once-both-stores-serve-170)).
