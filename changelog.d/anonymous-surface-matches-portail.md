### Security - without an account, Canari shows no more than the public showcase

A profile's memberships and roles, the shop, the category list, a form's agenda entry and the listing of recent posts now need a session; the public roster carries no account id; the page head no longer describes a profile or the agenda, and the sitemap lists no post. The single shared post's link preview stays ([anonymous-surface](docs/wiki/anonymous-surface.md)).
