### Fixed - Device lines, removal notices and call records are dated by their event, not by when the phone learnt of it

A device line takes the commit log instant of the commit that admitted the device (new members-only `GET /api/mls/commits/:groupId/accepted-at`), a removal notice the Remove commit's or the server's record of the seat's removal (new `GET /api/mls/groups/:groupId/own-removal`), a call record its frame's, with its duration on one clock; the acting device's notice and the archive replay now carry the server time too. With no durable instant the row is refused at ERROR, never clock-dated ([chat](docs/wiki/frontend/modules/chat.md#a-visible-notice-is-dated-by-its-frame-never-by-when-this-device-learnt-of-it)).
