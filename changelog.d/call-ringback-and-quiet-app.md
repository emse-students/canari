### Added - the caller hears a canary ringback, and the app's other sounds go quiet during a call

The caller now hears a soft canary phrase, looping, from the invite until the other side answers or the ring ends; from the ring (out or in) to the end of a call, message trills, audible media and notification sounds are silenced, while messages still arrive and show ([calls](docs/wiki/frontend/modules/calls.md#the-callers-ringback-2026-10-31)).
