### Added - the public agenda and its `.ics` can be narrowed to a campus and/or a formation (D40)

`feed` and `feed.ics` take `?campus=` and `?formation=`: only the events a matching space reaches, through the organiser or an accepted co-organiser. Neither parameter is the whole agenda, as before. See [profiles-and-access](docs/wiki/profiles-and-access.md#d40---the-anonymous-agenda-per-selection-as-built-2026-10-05).
