### Security - a BDE is managed by nobody but itself, and a BDE star edits an audience only inside its spaces

One BDE's whole-campus audience no longer makes the other BDE of its campus its super-admin; a BDE star reads every audience of its campus but adds or removes rules only inside its own spaces, a wider rule staying the platform admin's; an event proposal is told to the platform admins when a targeted space has no BDE; a member may leave an institution ([association-permissions](docs/wiki/association-permissions.md#the-grant-ceiling-who-may-write-which-bits-2026-11-02)).
