### Changed - the store reviewers' accounts are a cohort that is a space of its own

`SERVICE_ACCOUNT_USER_ID` now names a list of review accounts: they find, read and list only each other (search, directory, posts, lives, booking spaces), whatever their profile, and no member ever finds or reads them ([profiles-and-access](docs/wiki/profiles-and-access.md#the-review-cohort---store-reviewers-in-a-world-of-their-own-2026-10-31)).
