### Changed - the store reviewers' accounts are a cohort that is a space of its own

`SERVICE_ACCOUNT_USER_ID` now names a list of review accounts, and nothing crosses the cohort's boundary in either direction: search, profiles, posts, lives, follows, mentions, conversations, calls, the shop and booking spaces; an entry that is not a user id stops the service at boot ([profiles-and-access](docs/wiki/profiles-and-access.md#the-review-cohort---store-reviewers-in-a-world-of-their-own-2026-10-31)).
