### Changed - the in-app camera is the only camera, in chats and in the new-post composer

"Ouvrir la camera" opens Canari's own camera over the conversation or the post composer; a chat takes photos (sent as ordinary images), a post takes a photo or a video (post retention). The system camera is no longer reachable from the app, and its Android manifest entries are gone. See [reels-in-chat](docs/wiki/frontend/modules/reels-in-chat.md#1-entering-the-camera-from-a-conversation).
