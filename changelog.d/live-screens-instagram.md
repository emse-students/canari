### Changed - the live screens, redesigned in the manner of Instagram Live

Chat floats over the video without a box (avatar, name, shadow), scrolls, follows only from the bottom and shows a "new messages" chip; "Terminer" is a small confirmed pill at the top right beside Share and a chat toggle; the per-line eye-off became a tap menu (Report, Hide); "En direct" now closes the composer and ending the live returns to the feed ([live](docs/wiki/frontend/modules/live.md)).
