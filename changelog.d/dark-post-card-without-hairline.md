### Changed - the dark post card is separated from the page by lightness, not by a hairline

The 15 % white outline of a post card in the dark theme is gone: the card already stands 18 points of lightness off the OLED black, and Facebook's dark feed carries no outline either. Light is unchanged. The media-in-the-feed measurement of the same audit is recorded, with why the inset stays ([design-reference](docs/wiki/frontend/design-reference.md#22-the-post-card---facebooks-chrome-measured-on-the-same-phone-and-the-162px-it-gave-back)).
