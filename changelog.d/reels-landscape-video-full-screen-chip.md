### Added - a landscape CanaReel gets a "Plein ecran" button

A reel whose video file is wider than tall can be opened full screen, turned to landscape; closing
returns to the vertical pager on the same reel, at the same place in the video. Decided per video from
its own size
([reels](docs/wiki/frontend/modules/reels.md#a-landscape-file-gets-a-plein-ecran-chip-owner-2026-11-11)).
