### Fixed - a link ask reaches every online member at once, and a declined joiner's row goes

The ask and its outcome are announced by the server to every online admitted member, so the loser of an Add/Decline race sees the outcome without a refresh; a conversation the joiner was never admitted to no longer reads as an established channel and leaves the sidebar once declined ([mls-protocol](docs/wiki/protocols/mls-protocol.md#who-admits-a-new-person-2026-10-23)).
