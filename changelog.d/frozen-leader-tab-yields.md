### Fixed - a photo sent from a second tab stayed a placeholder until the hidden leader tab was shown

A hidden leader tab kept the lead and did not finish the send (Firefox), and a stored page then erased the photo's `pending`, so it drew the bare image placeholder. A visible tab with a queued message now asks a hidden leader for the lead and takes it, a Chromium-frozen leader releases it, a send resumed in a tab that stopped leading is refused at the encrypt, and a queued attachment says "queued" ([chat](docs/wiki/frontend/modules/chat.md#the-lead-goes-to-a-tab-that-can-serve-a-hidden-leader-hands-it-over-2026-11-10)).
