### Fixed - a pull that ends incomplete settles what waits on it, and a stalled resend after a 401 is asked smaller

A refused page or a page of one row that never arrives ends the pending pull typed, never as a drained mailbox, and that failed pull settles every drain wait at once so each caller decodes now or defers to the next complete pull; the resend after a 401 joins the page-halving ladder ([sessions](docs/wiki/sessions.md#a-401-renews-the-token-and-sends-the-same-request-once---one-rule-every-caller)).
