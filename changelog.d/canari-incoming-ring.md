### Changed - Canari rings with its own tone

An incoming call rings with "C1", two canaries in harmony chosen by the owner: on Android's new `canari_calls_v2` channel, through CallKit on the iPhone, and in the open app. On Android the open app now rings on the ringtone stream and honours silent and vibrate mode. It used to play a media-volume beep ([calls](docs/wiki/frontend/modules/calls.md#the-incoming-ring-canaris-own-owner-2026-11-01)).
