### Added - an institution's audience can be a union of campuses and formations

The creation form and, for a global admin, the Audience tab of any entity have a **Custom** choice: tick the campuses and formations, written as the spaces grid would write them. The other creation gaps were already closed or are by design, see [associations](docs/wiki/frontend/modules/associations.md#the-audience-tab-and-the-campus-prompt-wp-b-2026-10-08).
