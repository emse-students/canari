### Changed - seat claims and departures are serialised under one lock, and `POST /mls/commit` is rate-limited per user

Every write that creates, promotes, routes, queues to or evicts a seat - a Welcome's store included - shares one per-group lock and a per-group seat generation (chat-delivery migration 036), with no HTTP call under the lock; an account erasure cuts those seats under the lock after social-service's part and deletes the account's eviction rows. A commit advances the epoch only from the base the gate read. Every key-group departure takes one path that records its outcome (social migration 108) and settles an abandoned one at once. The commit route answers `429` with its wait past 600 commits per user per minute ([channel-encryption](docs/wiki/protocols/channel-encryption.md#wp-21-and-the-gate-it-had-nowhere-to-live)).
