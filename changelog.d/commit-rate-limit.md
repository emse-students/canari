### Changed - seat claims and departures are serialised under one lock, and `POST /mls/commit` is rate-limited per user

Every write that creates, promotes, routes, queues to or evicts a seat - a Welcome's store included - shares one per-group lock and the `dm_seat_evictions` row (chat-delivery migration 036), with no HTTP call under the lock; an account erasure clears that row after social-service's part. Every key-group departure takes one path that records its outcome (social migration 108) and settles an abandoned one at once. The commit route answers `429` with its wait past 600 commits per user per minute ([channel-encryption](docs/wiki/protocols/channel-encryption.md#wp-21-and-the-gate-it-had-nowhere-to-live)).
