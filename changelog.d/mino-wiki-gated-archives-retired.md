### Changed - MinoWiki is gated like MiGallery and Le Cercle, and Archives MINO is retired from MiConnect

MiConnect lets a person into MinoWiki under the same rule as MiGallery and Le Cercle (the free window, a post, an Alumni contribution this year, or the group `acces-mino-wiki`), and its provider offers no refresh grant. The Archives MINO application and provider are deleted at the next stable, and signing out of the wiki returns to the wiki instead of the archives ([mino-access](docs/wiki/infrastructure/mino-access.md#archives-mino-is-retired-2026-11-27)).
