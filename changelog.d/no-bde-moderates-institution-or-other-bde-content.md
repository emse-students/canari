### Fixed - no BDE moderates a comment in an institution's or another BDE's name

A comment published as an institution or another BDE could still be deleted, or its writer read, by
the BDE moderating the post it sat under; only the platform admin may now, as for posts and lives, and
a refusal is worded by its code ([moderation-and-blocking](docs/wiki/moderation-and-blocking.md#who-may-read-the-queue)).
