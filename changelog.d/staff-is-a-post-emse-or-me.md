### Changed - "Personnel" reaches every EMSE or ME post holder, students included

An institution's "Personnel" public now reaches an account holding the MiConnect post EMSE or ME, with or without a cursus, instead of every account without a cursus; an alumnus who works at the School reads both student and staff posts ([profiles-and-access](docs/wiki/profiles-and-access.md#staff-is-a-post-emse-or-me-owner-2026-11-24)).
