### Changed - a mention banner names the person from the phone's own names

A native mention notification now shows the mentioned person's name from the names this device already resolved, mirrored by the app into `display_names.json`, and still says "someone" for a name it never knew; nothing new rides the push ([mobile](docs/wiki/frontend/mobile.md#a-mention-names-its-person-from-the-phones-own-copy-wp-mention-name-2026-10-18)).
