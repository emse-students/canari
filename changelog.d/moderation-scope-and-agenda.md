### Changed - a BDE moderates its own spaces, only the author edits a post, an institution is the admin's to delete, an association's agenda is closed to anonymous callers

`MODERATE` is scoped to the spaces the BDE governs; nobody, the platform admin included, edits another person's post or comment; deleting an institution needs the global admin; `GET /api/associations/:id/events` answers an anonymous caller only for an association that addresses everyone ([D23 amended](docs/wiki/profiles-and-access.md#d23-amended---moderate-is-scoped-and-only-the-author-edits-a-post-2026-11-02), [D40 closed](docs/wiki/profiles-and-access.md#d40-closed---one-associations-agenda-is-closed-to-an-anonymous-caller-2026-11-02)).
