### Changed - a new booking space has no longest booking, and the editor shows the field

A space created without a limit no longer caps a booking at 2 h: "Durée maximale d'une réservation" sits in the booking rules, empty means no limit, and the duration it allows is shown live. Migration 151 relaxes one CHECK; existing spaces keep their value ([bookings](docs/wiki/bookings-chantier.md)).
