### Changed - opening the app on Android no longer clears banners nobody read

A message, mention or reaction banner now goes only with its conversation - opened here, read
elsewhere, answered, or ended (deleted, left, excluded) - as in Messenger and Signal (owner, 2026-11-21).
Call rings and the notices that ask to open the app still go when it opens. iOS follows in its own
entry ([social-service](docs/wiki/services/social-service.md)).
