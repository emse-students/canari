### Fixed - a tag counts only for the association that issued it

A raw tag grant accepts only one of the granting association's own tiers, a membership product's tag is always derived, never typed, a grant never renews a row another association issued, tier-holding reads and the sibling-tier swap count only the tier's own association's tags, and no new slug or tier key may make two associations' tag names coincide ([cotisations](docs/wiki/cotisations.md#a-tag-counts-only-for-the-association-that-issued-it)).
