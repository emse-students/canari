### Added - voice notes play near one loudness

A voice note's loudness is measured on the sender's device with the video meter, and every reader's
player brings it toward the same target through the same Web Audio route, on iPhone too; older notes
play as before ([video-player](docs/wiki/frontend/modules/video-player.md#voice-notes-take-the-same-loudness-owner-2026-11-12)).
