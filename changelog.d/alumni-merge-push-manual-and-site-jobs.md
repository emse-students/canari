### Added - the Alumni merge completes on MiConnect's push, can be done by hand, and follows on the sibling sites

MiConnect now pushes each completed merge to Canari, and its journal line survives the duplicate's deletion. An admin can attach an Alumni account to an account of another name with a written reason. A completed merge queues one job per sibling site ([profiles-and-access](docs/wiki/profiles-and-access.md#the-alumni-merge-extended-d52-d58-planned-for-183)).
