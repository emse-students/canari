### Fixed - iOS: a tap on a push banner opens its conversation from a backgrounded or killed app

On every launch the notification plugin took over the iOS notification delegate and dropped every push tap. Canari now takes the delegate back at launch. A tap waits for the page in durable state and is routed once the router is mounted. A reply typed in a notification over an open or backgrounded app is now handed to the page, sealed and tied to its account, and the process waits until the page owns it, so it is no longer lost ([mobile](docs/wiki/frontend/mobile.md#one-notification-delegate-and-a-tap-that-waits-for-the-page-2026-11-01)).
