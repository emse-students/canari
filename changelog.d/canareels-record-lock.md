### Added - CanaReels: slide to the padlock to film hands-free

While holding the shutter, sliding the thumb onto the padlock to its left and lifting keeps the take recording; one tap on the stop button ends it. A plain hold-and-release and a short tap behave as before ([reels](docs/wiki/frontend/modules/reels.md#slide-to-lock-owner-2026-11-27)).
