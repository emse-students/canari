### Changed - an answer on the call ring shows the call at once and joins without waiting for every conversation

Answering on the ring now shows the call screen straight away, connecting, and the join waits only for the call's own conversation to be up to date - which is caught up first - instead of every conversation's messages (10.7 s on a resumed Android phone) ([calls](docs/wiki/frontend/modules/calls.md#the-answer-joins-on-its-own-group-184-2026-11-28)).
