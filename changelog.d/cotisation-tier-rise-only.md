### Fixed - a cotisation tier is bought only as a rise over the one held

A holder of a tier can no longer buy a cheaper, equally priced or unrankable sibling tier, which used to replace theirs, and the preferential price of a rise keeps applying across the academic-year rollover; the listings now say where each tier stands and what it would charge ([cotisations](docs/wiki/cotisations.md#rising-to-a-higher-tier)).
