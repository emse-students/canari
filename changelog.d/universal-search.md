### Added - one search for the whole app (1.8.4)

The home page's search field becomes ONE search across people, conversations and their messages, communities, associations, posts (with the person and association filters), events, forms and settings, opened on a phone by the header's magnifier from every tab; messages are searched on the device only, and the chat sidebar's filter is gone. [Design](docs/wiki/frontend/universal-search.md).
