### Security - a member manager grants and revokes only the rights it holds itself

A granter could grant association flags it did not hold; an ordinary member is now capped at its own flags, an institution's members are the platform admin's alone, and a BDE super-admin's writes of the rights it does not inherit are audited ([association-permissions](docs/wiki/association-permissions.md#the-grant-ceiling-who-may-write-which-bits-2026-11-02)).
