### Security - a member manager grants, revokes and removes only within the rights it holds itself

A granter could grant association flags it did not hold; an ordinary member is now capped at its own flags (removing a member included), an institution's members are the platform admin's alone, a BDE writes audiences only within the spaces it governs, and a BDE super-admin's writes of the rights it does not inherit are audited ([association-permissions](docs/wiki/association-permissions.md#the-grant-ceiling-who-may-write-which-bits-2026-11-02)).
