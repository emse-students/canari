### Fixed - accepting a co-organisation on a validated event sends it back to validation

An acceptance widens the event's audience with nothing validated behind the new part, so the event returns to pending in the acceptance's own transaction. The validators are notified as for a proposal, and the organiser and the partners are told why, as they now are when a date moves ([profiles-and-access](docs/wiki/profiles-and-access.md#wp-q2-an-acceptance-on-a-validated-event-reopens-it-2026-11-03)).
