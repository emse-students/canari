### Fixed - tapping a MiGallery link in a message three times no longer opens it three times

The sign-in handoff keeps the opens still minting, drops a second tap on the same one and shows a toast that the tap was taken, so a slow mint no longer launches the browser three times (the app ending after it is not explained yet).
