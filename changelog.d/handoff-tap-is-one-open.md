### Fixed - tapping a MiGallery link in a message three times no longer opens it three times and closes the app

The sign-in handoff keeps the opens still minting and drops a second tap on the same one, so a slow
mint no longer launches the browser three times. See [sessions](docs/wiki/sessions.md#one-tap-is-one-open-the-handoff-has-an-in-flight-guard-owners-log-of-2026-11-27-184).
