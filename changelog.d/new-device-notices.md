### Security - a new device on your account is announced

The owner is alerted in the app and, from the phone's background engine, by a local notification; a contact's new device is a discreet line in every shared DM or group, never in a community. A device of your own that peers refuse is told to you with its remedy. All come from the signed MLS commit or this device's own refusal, never from the server. See [mls-protocol](docs/wiki/protocols/mls-protocol.md#new-device-notices-decided-by-the-user-2026-10-22-refined-the-same-day).
