### Added - "Envoyer a..." on the camera tab: a take can go to several conversations at once (off until the chat writer switch turns on)

After a video on the CanaReels camera, the review offers a sheet - "Publier sur CanaReels" first, then DMs and groups, with search and a server-capped multi-select - that queues ONE take with one send per conversation, and hands the publish step's upload to the chats when both are ticked. Inert while `CHAT_REEL_WRITER_ENABLED` is off ([reels-in-chat](docs/wiki/frontend/modules/reels-in-chat.md#18-what-the-send-to-sheet-rc-12-built-2026-11-02)).
