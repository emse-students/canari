### Added - a Minesweeper tutorial with playable mini-grids and an "Indice" button

`/games/tutorial` teaches opening, flagging, the chord and the 1-1, 1-2-1 and 1-2-2-1 patterns on small
grids that run on the real engine; the hint button rings the numbers it reads and tints the cells they
speak about, and every lesson is proved by enumeration rather than read
([tutorial](docs/wiki/frontend/modules/minesweeper.md#the-tutorial-owner-2026-11-25)).
