### Added - a hint bulb in an unranked Minesweeper game

In a "Libre" game, a bulb lights the number(s) that justify the next certain move - never the move - a misplaced flag, or the mines-left counter when only the total decides; a ranked game has no bulb
([minesweeper](docs/wiki/frontend/modules/minesweeper.md#the-bulb-a-hint-in-an-unranked-game-owner-2026-11-25)).
