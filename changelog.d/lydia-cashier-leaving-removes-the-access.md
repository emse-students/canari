### Fixed - a Lydia cashier who leaves the association loses their Lydia access

When a member leaves or is removed, their Tresorier or Vendeur numbers are removed at Lydia through the verified removal path, retried each time a manager opens the payments card; a Responsable who leaves keeps the Business's number and the card says a new one is needed. Deleting an association is refused while its Lydia Business still lists cashiers to remove ([payments](docs/wiki/frontend/modules/payments.md#the-cashiers-enrolment-lot-3)).
