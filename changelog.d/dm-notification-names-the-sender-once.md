### Fixed - a direct-message notification names its sender once

On Android 11+ the system titles a conversation notification with its shortcut, so a direct message in the group shape showed the name in the header and again above the line; it now takes the one-to-one shape there, and the reader's own inline reply stays named. On Android and iOS, an unreadable direct message now reads "Nouveau message" under the sender instead of repeating the name ([mobile](docs/wiki/frontend/mobile.md)).
