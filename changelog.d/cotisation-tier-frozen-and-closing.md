### Fixed - a tier rise is settled on what its checkout decided, and a payment not granted can be closed

A cotisation rise is granted on the tier the buyer held when the checkout opened, so a price edited before the payment lands no longer leaves it ungranted; a payment paid for and not granted can be marked refunded or granted from the association's purchases, and the hourly report then stops counting it ([cotisations](docs/wiki/cotisations.md#rising-to-a-higher-tier)).
