### Fixed - Android: a notification posted while the app was open now goes when the conversation is read on another device

The WebView now hands every own read it learns over its socket to the native side, which records it (a late post of a read message is refused) and removes the banner once the read covers every line it holds; a salon's `channel_read` push acts while the app is in front ([social-service](docs/wiki/services/social-service.md)). A banner answered inline now leaves the shade on Android 15+, and the background resend waits for a network before it counts an attempt ([chat-delivery](docs/wiki/services/chat-delivery.md)).
