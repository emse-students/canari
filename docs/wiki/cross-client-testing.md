# Cross-client testing - the board

**STATE ONLY, AND IN AS FEW WORDS AS THE STATE ALLOWS.** A cell is a verdict, a date and the build
it ran on. **A `PASS` cell says nothing else** - not the numbers, not how the row was fixed, not what
it read PASS-DIRTY on first. They were here for 117 cells and made the file a third longer than the
state it carries, in violation of this very paragraph. Where each of them belongs instead:
`results.ndjson` holds the measurement behind every cell, the runner's own source holds the reason
for every instrument fix, [testing-methodology](testing-methodology.md) holds the rules those fixes
distilled into, `CHANGELOG.md` holds every product defect,
[cross-client-campaign](cross-client-campaign.md) holds the campaign's shape and cost,
[the README](../../tools/cross-client-harness/README.md) is the instrument, and `CLAUDE.md` is what
is open. **A cell that is not `PASS` may carry prose, because what it is waiting on is state.**

Two accounts, **owner** (W1, W3, A1) and **peer** (W2). **Target is the LOCAL estate.** **Run order
is the numbered ladder below, top to bottom - the only copy of that order.**

**`bun rows.mjs` IS WHAT SETTLES THIS FILE, AND IT HAS CAUGHT THE BOARD WRONG FIVE TIMES.** It reads
the ledger, adjudicates a COMPARISON row on ALL its halves, and names every cell the ledger
contradicts. Run it before believing anything below. The numbers in `Standing` are its output on the
date that table names and nowhere else - they are a snapshot, not a source.

**THE BOARD WAS RESET TO ZERO ON 2026-09-03** (decision 12 of the
[workflow migration](workflow-migration.md#2-the-twelve-decisions---decided-not-to-be-relitigated)):
the rig behind the previous verdicts was collected without `-WithRig`, so its ledger, both Chrome
profiles and the phone baseline are gone, and a verdict whose run cannot be read is a claim with
nothing behind it. The target also moved from production to LOCAL and the accounts changed, which
would have invalidated much of it anyway. The old board is kept verbatim at
[cross-client-testing-archive](cross-client-testing-archive.md). **Two things it is still good for
and one it is not**: it says which mechanisms held once and on which build, and it is where to look
FIRST when a re-run disagrees with itself - it is worth exactly nothing as a gate.

**A `pending` cell that carries a note KEEPS the note.** Those are EXPECTATIONS written with the row
("the AEAD tag must fail"), not verdicts, and they are what the row is for.
