2. **A form's waiting list: a late payment over capacity keeps the person's place** (confirmed). The payment of a person who is waiting, arriving after the form is full, keeps their place in the queue; at promotion it BECOMES their registration. The payer is refunded only if they are never promoted. It applies to G-WAITLIST, planned in [the forms gaps entry](../backlog.d/p2-forms-the-gaps-left-after-the-priced-grid.md) (`1.10.0`) and designed with the audit's Q6 ([forms-capability-audit](../forms-capability-audit-2026-11-06.md)).
3. **Two readings the bench cannot take are CLOSED**: a Canari call started during a cellular call, and the microphone coming back by itself when the cellular call ends. The bench Mi 9T has no SIM; `CallFocusHoldTest` and `CallAudioSlotTest` cover the focus hold and the audio slot. The same rule as [2026-11-23](owner-decisions-of-2026-11-23.md), item 1. Where: [calls](../frontend/modules/calls.md), [device-verification](../device-verification.md#readings-closed-on-2026-11-24---the-bench-has-no-sim).
4. **Tabs: fix the symptom in `1.8.0`, design the browser-wide worker now, build it in `1.9.0`** (owner, 2026-11-24, after a screenshot of a second tab refusing "Nouvelle discussion" with "L'onglet principal de Canari n'est pas prêt"). The `1.8.0` fix is architectural: the lead belongs to a tab whose session can serve it, and is given up from the session's own flags, never after a timeout ([chat](../frontend/modules/chat.md#a-leader-whose-own-session-ended-gives-the-lead-up-2026-11-24)). The `SharedWorker` that replaces the leader tab is designed in [its entry](../backlog.d/a-browser-wide-worker-holds-the-mls-state-instead-of-a-leader-tab.md) and built in `1.9.0` under an adversarial review. Charter principle 2, *the essential is never lost* ([principes](../../principes.md)): a gesture must not dead-end because another tab went to sleep.
5. **A campus-trend notification for a post this device already displayed shows nothing - decided on the DEVICE** (owner, 2026-11-24, choice "Verif sur l'appareil"). A "Suggestion : ..." can announce a post the member already saw in the feed, because the server records no "seen" and must not (charter principles 1 and 3, [principes](../../principes.md)). The device keeps a local record of the posts it displayed; a trend for one of them shows NO banner and NO bell row, on every surface, and NOTHING new is sent to the server. The accepted limit: a post seen on another device is not recognised. Where: [engagement](../engagement.md#a-campus-trend-for-a-post-this-device-already-displayed-shows-nothing-2026-11-24), the register line [C32](../compliance/register-of-processing.md), the blank passive row iOS leaves is removed by the app (the extension stamps it, the app deletes it at each foreground; dropping the push itself needs Apple's filtering entitlement, an owner action to apply for), the reading owed in [the entry](../backlog.d/reading-owed-a-campus-trend-for-a-displayed-post-shows-nothing.md).
