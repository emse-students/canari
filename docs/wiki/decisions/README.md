# decisions - the records of what the owner decided

These files are RECORDS, not work: each says what was decided, on which day, and where the detail
lives. They carry no `target:` line and do not appear in [plan](../plan.md); an open work item
found in one of them is its own entry in [backlog.d](../backlog.d/README.md) (moved there on
2026-11-23, when the records left the backlog).

- `owner-decisions-of-<date>.md` - the owner's answers of one day, numbered, each line naming its
  detail. The latest is [2026-11-26, the Alumni merge](owner-decisions-of-2026-11-26-alumni.md). A decision already asked is
  never asked again.
- `decided-by-the-user-on-<date>-*.md` - the earlier records of 2026-10-18 and 2026-10-23, trimmed to
  what was not written elsewhere.
- [group-calls-p2p-mesh-up-to-4-audio-3-video-decided-2026-10-31.md](group-calls-p2p-mesh-up-to-4-audio-3-video-decided-2026-10-31.md)
  - the design and decisions of the group calls chantier.
- [audience-audit-2026-11-14.md](audience-audit-2026-11-14.md) - the audit of the audience model and
  the owner's answers; its open findings are [an entry](../backlog.d/p2-the-audience-audit-of-2026-11-14-left-these-open.md).

A new record is a file here, linked from the page that applies it, with no `target:` line.
