## The owner's decisions of 2026-11-25

Recorded so no question answered here is asked again.

1. **"@tous" mentions every member of a group or a community salon, in `1.8.2`** (owner, 2026-11-25, the day `1.8.0` shipped). Today a mention names one person (one `@[uuid]` token per member); there was no way to call a whole conversation. Where: groups AND community salons. Who may write it: in a group every member (everyone in a group is equal); in a community salon admins and moderators only (a member must not wake a whole year group). Notification: it counts as a mention - a member at "Mentions" is notified, "Aucune" still silences everything. The server sees nothing: the device recognises the reserved token after decrypting, so the restriction is enforced on both ends (the composer, and every receiving device against the roster it holds). Charter principle 1, *your messages are yours* ([principes](../../principes.md)): the server learns nothing new. Where: [the entry](../backlog.d/mention-everyone-in-groups-and-salons.md).

2. **The self conversation shows the user's OWN avatar and "Name (Notes)", in `1.8.1`** (owner, 2026-11-25). "Mes notes" no longer carries its label and its bookmark icon: the row, the header, the pickers and the notification name it with the account's own name followed by a localized suffix ("Prenom NOM (Notes)"), drawn with the account's avatar (initials when it has none). It is found by any part of the name AND by "notes" / "mes notes" / "my notes", kept as search aliases. Pinned position and every behaviour unchanged. [amended by item 9] Where: [chat](../frontend/modules/chat.md#mes-notes---the-conversation-with-oneself-2026-11-22).

3. **The notepad migration and the removal of the profile notepad page (`/profile/me/notepad`) move forward to `1.8.2`** (owner, 2026-11-25), from `1.9.0`. Where: [the entry](../backlog.d/mes-notes-notepad-migration-and-retention.md).

4. **One global search bar is planned for `1.9.0`** (owner, 2026-11-25, from feedback he received). People want ONE search across posts (the author, the content AND the comments), conversations and groups, users (replacing the directory), booking rooms and the rest of the app's objects. Whether it complements or replaces the posts search bar is open; the owner discusses the design when `1.9.0` starts. Constraint: conversations and messages are end-to-end encrypted (charter principle 1), so their search runs on the device only. Where: [the entry](../backlog.d/a-global-search-bar.md).

5. **A member may send their history to a member newly added to a private group (owner, 2026-11-25)**, in `1.8.2`. Asked from a WhatsApp screenshot: under the "X a rejoint le groupe" notice, a button "Envoyer l'historique des messages". Any member may send it (a group has no admins): they send the messages THEIR device holds, from a date they choose (shortcuts 24 h, 7 days, 30 days, everything, or a date). Every member then sees a notice, "A a envoyé à X l'historique des messages échangés depuis : <date>", with an "En savoir plus" link. No group setting forbids it: it is always possible. End to end, device to device: the existing history bundle path, given a start date; the server sees nothing. **An explicit, visible exception to [the 2026-10-21 decision](../protocols/history-reconciliation.md#a-new-member-receives-nothing-from-before-their-arrival-decided-by-the-user-2026-10-21) that a new member receives nothing from before their arrival**: that default stays, and nothing is sent unless a member presses the button. Charter principle 1, *your messages are yours* ([principes](../../principes.md)): sending hands over the OTHER members' messages too, and the notice every member sees is what makes that acceptable. Private group conversations only: communities keep their own `shared`/`joined` setting, and a 1:1 conversation has no add path. Where: [the entry](../backlog.d/send-history-to-a-new-group-member.md).

6. **Minesweeper: the record date reads DD/MM/YY (`1.8.1`, #4090), and an unranked game gets a hint bulb (`1.8.2`)** (owner, 2026-11-25). *"En mode non classe (libre), j'aimerais bien qu'on puisse avoir un peu de didacticiel pour s'entrainer"*. The bulb's four answers: (1) it lights the number(s) that justify the next certain move - one when it suffices, the fewest to read together otherwise - and NEVER the cell to play; (2) a wrong flag comes first, lit and said to be misplaced; (3) when only the mine total decides, the mines-left counter is lit; (4) unranked games only - a ranked game has no bulb and nothing the server replays changes. **The `1.8.2` target is held by merge timing**: the pull request stays a draft until the `1.8.1` stable is cut, and the reading entry's `target:` moved to `1.8.2` when `1.8.1` shipped (2026-11-25). Charter principle 3, *your attention is not for sale* ([principes](../../principes.md)): the bulb is asked for, never pushed. Where: [minesweeper](../frontend/modules/minesweeper.md#the-bulb-a-hint-in-an-unranked-game-owner-2026-11-25), [the reading](../backlog.d/reading-owed-the-minesweeper-hint.md).

7. **Three levels in an unranked game, in `1.8.2`** (owner, 2026-11-25: *"le demineur est vraiment super dur, la barriere a l'entree est terrifiante ... sans remettre en cause le niveau du classe"*). The answers: (1) Libre offers Debutant 9x9 / 10 mines, Intermediaire 16x16 / 40 mines and Expert, the ranked board (18x32 / 150, generator 2); ranked stays exactly as it is, and the picker exists in Libre only, locked once the first cell is dug; (2) the two smaller levels are guess-free with simple deductions - generator 1, which stops at the first layout its solver clears, with no reasoning climb; neither generator, nor any fingerprint, nor the server changes; (3) no undo after a mine - losing stays as it is; (4) the level is a per-device setting, validated on read: with none stored, a player who already chose ranked or Libre on the device starts on Expert, a newcomer on Debutant. Shipped in the same pull request as the bulb, held to `1.8.2` the same way. Charter principle 7, *simple* ([principes](../../principes.md)): a board a newcomer can finish. **[amended, same day, by the owner: *"priorise les difficultes par generation et pas par taille"*]** The levels are NOT sizes: every level plays the ranked board's size (18x32 / 150) on generator 2 and differs only by the reasoning score the generator climbs to (Debutant 0, Intermediaire 30, Expert 60 = the ranked target), the number never shown; points (1) and (2) above are replaced accordingly, (3) and (4) stand. Where: [minesweeper](../frontend/modules/minesweeper.md#three-levels-in-an-unranked-game-owner-2026-11-25).

8. **One sign-in per identity on the MiConnect paths; the routing page links without a second Alumnforce sign-in when the names agree (owner, 2026-11-25, option 1 of three)**, in `1.8.2`. Asked from a real enrolment: after the Alumni button and "J'ai un compte de l'École qui fonctionne", the person met the login page again, the CAS, a confirmation page, Alumnforce a SECOND time, MiConnect's settings, and had to sign in to the application again. Now: the identity Alumnforce asserted in that first sign-in is kept server-side in the session and, after the CAS sign-in, linked to the CAS account with no second Alumnforce sign-in - at once when the asserted name equals the account's name (folded by the one comparison the D51 merge uses), after a page naming BOTH accounts and a tick otherwise - with the membership read of that assertion (D43); then the SAME application authorization resumes. The link step (D44) also resumes the application and no longer shows the D49 page (D49 is "every link outside the D44 step"). Settings > Sources "Connect" is unchanged. **The residual risk, accepted knowingly: a homonym** - someone who walks away from a shared browser after "J'ai un compte de l'École" and a person of the SAME name who then signs in with the CAS there are linked with no page; any other name meets the page naming both. The second Alumnforce sign-in was the only proof that the person at the keyboard after the CAS still held that Alumni identity; the owner traded it for the fluid path. Charter principle 7, *simple, léger, agréable* ([principes](../../principes.md)), and principle 1 untouched (no message content is involved). Where: [miconnect-sign-in-paths](../infrastructure/miconnect-sign-in-paths.md).

9. **"Mes notes" follows the same chronological rule as every conversation, in `1.8.2`** (owner, 2026-11-25). It is no longer drawn above the list: its place is its last activity (`compareConversationRecency`), and the search filter is the common one (its aliases kept). **A correction of the record:** the owner never asked for it to be pinned at the top; the "(owner, 2026-11-22)" written in `Sidebar.svelte`'s comment was a misattribution (the 2026-11-22 decisions file records the conversation, not a pin), and it is removed with the pin. **Where this device holds no Notes conversation yet** (the first tap creates it), the entry is drawn at the END of the list until it has activity - it has no recency, so it stays discoverable without jumping to the top; a reversible default chosen by the lead. Where: [chat](../frontend/modules/chat.md#mes-notes---the-conversation-with-oneself-2026-11-22).

10. **A sibling site opens signed in more often: through its MiConnect sign-in when MiConnect has a page to show first, and from a link in a message (owner, 2026-11-25)**, in `1.8.2`. Measured on production the same morning: of 27 MiGallery mints in four hours, 21 were refused `terms_not_accepted` (everyone who accepted MiConnect's terms before the text of 2026-11-24, the owner included) and the site opened plainly, signed out. (1) A mint refused for a page MiConnect must show first (the terms, the Alumni link step, the Alumni gate) opens the site's own MiConnect sign-in entry, declared per site in `libs/contracts/ecosystem-sites.json` (`signIn`), with the destination as the return target when it is on the site's allowlist; the other refusals keep the plain open. (2) A link to a site whose handoff is on, tapped in a message or a post, is handed off when its path is on the site's `dests` allowlist, after the Safe Browsing gate - reversing the "never from links inside content" choice of 2026-11-07, bounded by the allowlist and the sites' step-up (the abuse analysis is section 3.7 of the design note). Charter principle 7, *simple, léger, agréable* ([principes](../../principes.md)): one tap, signed in; principle 1 untouched: the client matches the link locally, and the server learns the tapped path (which it already sees through the Safe Browsing lookup), never the message; its audit line names an album by its pattern, `/albums/{uuid}`, never by its id. Where: [sessions](../sessions.md#the-sign-in-handoff-to-the-miconnect-sites-2026-11-08), [the entry](../backlog.d/sign-in-handoff-to-the-ecosystem-sites-and-migallery-notifications.md).
