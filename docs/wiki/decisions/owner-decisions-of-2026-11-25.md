## The owner's decisions of 2026-11-25

Recorded so no question answered here is asked again.

1. **"@tous" mentions every member of a group or a community salon, in `1.8.2`** (owner, 2026-11-25, the day `1.8.0` shipped). Today a mention names one person (one `@[uuid]` token per member); there was no way to call a whole conversation. Where: groups AND community salons. Who may write it: in a group every member (everyone in a group is equal); in a community salon admins and moderators only (a member must not wake a whole year group). Notification: it counts as a mention - a member at "Mentions" is notified, "Aucune" still silences everything. The server sees nothing: the device recognises the reserved token after decrypting, so the restriction is enforced on both ends (the composer, and every receiving device against the roster it holds). Charter principle 1, *your messages are yours* ([principes](../../principes.md)): the server learns nothing new. Where: [the entry](../backlog.d/mention-everyone-in-groups-and-salons.md).

2. **The self conversation shows the user's OWN avatar and "Name (Notes)", in `1.8.1`** (owner, 2026-11-25). "Mes notes" no longer carries its label and its bookmark icon: the row, the header, the pickers and the notification name it with the account's own name followed by a localized suffix ("Prenom NOM (Notes)"), drawn with the account's avatar (initials when it has none). It is found by any part of the name AND by "notes" / "mes notes" / "my notes", kept as search aliases. Pinned position and every behaviour unchanged. Where: [chat](../frontend/modules/chat.md#mes-notes---the-conversation-with-oneself-2026-11-22).

3. **The notepad migration and the removal of the profile notepad page (`/profile/me/notepad`) move forward to `1.8.2`** (owner, 2026-11-25), from `1.9.0`. Where: [the entry](../backlog.d/mes-notes-notepad-migration-and-retention.md).

4. **One global search bar is planned for `1.9.0`** (owner, 2026-11-25, from feedback he received). People want ONE search across posts (the author, the content AND the comments), conversations and groups, users (replacing the directory), booking rooms and the rest of the app's objects. Whether it complements or replaces the posts search bar is open; the owner discusses the design when `1.9.0` starts. Constraint: conversations and messages are end-to-end encrypted (charter principle 1), so their search runs on the device only. Where: [the entry](../backlog.d/a-global-search-bar.md).

5. **A member may send their history to a member newly added to a private group (owner, 2026-11-25)**, in `1.8.2`. Asked from a WhatsApp screenshot: under the "X a rejoint le groupe" notice, a button "Envoyer l'historique des messages". Any member may send it (a group has no admins): they send the messages THEIR device holds, from a date they choose (shortcuts 24 h, 7 days, 30 days, everything, or a date). Every member then sees a notice, "A a envoyé à X l'historique des messages échangés depuis : <date>", with an "En savoir plus" link. No group setting forbids it: it is always possible. End to end, device to device: the existing history bundle path, given a start date; the server sees nothing. **An explicit, visible exception to [the 2026-10-21 decision](../protocols/history-reconciliation.md#a-new-member-receives-nothing-from-before-their-arrival-decided-by-the-user-2026-10-21) that a new member receives nothing from before their arrival**: that default stays, and nothing is sent unless a member presses the button. Charter principle 1, *your messages are yours* ([principes](../../principes.md)): sending hands over the OTHER members' messages too, and the notice every member sees is what makes that acceptable. Private group conversations only: communities keep their own `shared`/`joined` setting, and a 1:1 conversation has no add path. Where: [the entry](../backlog.d/send-history-to-a-new-group-member.md).

6. **One sign-in per identity on the MiConnect paths; the routing page links without a second Alumnforce sign-in when the names agree (owner, 2026-11-25, option 1 of three)**, in `1.8.2`. Asked from a real enrolment: after the Alumni button and "J'ai un compte de l'École qui fonctionne", the person met the login page again, the CAS, a confirmation page, Alumnforce a SECOND time, MiConnect's settings, and had to sign in to the application again. Now: the identity Alumnforce asserted in that first sign-in is kept server-side in the session and, after the CAS sign-in, linked to the CAS account with no second Alumnforce sign-in - at once when the asserted name equals the account's name (folded by the one comparison the D51 merge uses), after a page naming BOTH accounts and a tick otherwise - with the membership read of that assertion (D43); then the SAME application authorization resumes. The link step (D44) also resumes the application and no longer shows the D49 page (D49 is "every link outside the D44 step"). Settings > Sources "Connect" is unchanged. **The residual risk, accepted knowingly: a homonym** - someone who walks away from a shared browser after "J'ai un compte de l'École" and a person of the SAME name who then signs in with the CAS there are linked with no page; any other name meets the page naming both. The second Alumnforce sign-in was the only proof that the person at the keyboard after the CAS still held that Alumni identity; the owner traded it for the fluid path. Charter principle 7, *simple, léger, agréable* ([principes](../../principes.md)), and principle 1 untouched (no message content is involved). Where: [miconnect-sign-in-paths](../infrastructure/miconnect-sign-in-paths.md).
