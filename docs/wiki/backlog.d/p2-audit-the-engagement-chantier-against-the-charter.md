## P2 - audit the engagement chantier against the charter (to run after the 1.6.10 stable, owner, 2026-11-10)

The engagement chantier ([engagement](../engagement.md), [backlog](engagement-chantier.md)) was
built before [Nos principes](../../principes.md) was written. After the `1.6.10` stable, audit what it
ships against the charter, principle by principle, and file what does not hold:

- **Principle 2 (the essential is never lost)**: Pour toi's ranking and its suggested posts, the
  suggestion carousel and the campus trends must never push a followed person's or an association's
  post out of reach; Campus and Associations stay chronological with nothing inserted.
- **Principle 3 (your attention is not for sale)**: every suggestion and every notification type
  (the bell, trends, openings, friends' naming) switches off in one gesture; the daily and weekly
  caps hold on production counts; no time-spent measure exists anywhere (check the `[ENGAGEMENT]`
  metrics and the activity-sharing columns); every ranking is explainable in one sentence and a
  chronological view exists beside it; no streak, no "X saw your profile".
- **Principle 1**: no engagement signal reads or derives anything from message content.

The deliverable is a table of claim, code path, measurement and verdict on the wiki, and a backlog
entry per failure. Nothing here changes behaviour before that audit.
