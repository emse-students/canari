**Built 2026-11-16, owed one iPhone reading** ([offline-and-weak-network 13.9](../frontend/offline-and-weak-network.md#139-a-queued-file-over-one-request-body-resumes-and-its-figure-never-goes-back-2026-11-16)):
- **The drop was a real restart from byte 0.** Read from the code: a non-reel chat file took the legacy chunk route (a fresh uploadId per attempt, 8 MiB chunks), and the outbox wrote `loaded: 0` at every hold and attempt start. What ended the first attempt (the 45 s idle guard, a broken connection or an abort) is NOT known, because the in-app log was not exported. A queued file over 8 MiB now goes through a resumable session (1 MiB parts, a second in flight once a part has committed, the session recorded in the outbox row). A cut or a kill resends only the parts the server lacks. The bubble's figure never goes back.
- **The rate is the link, not our pacing.** Measured in Chrome against dev with the same routes: unthrottled 3.2 to 5.5 MB/s, and on "Fast 3G" every shape ran at the link's 77 to 80 KB/s. The phone's own rate is now logged per round (`[UPLOAD-RESUME] ... KB/s`).

**The reading that closes it (iPhone 12, the first pre-release carrying the fix):** queue a 13 MB file offline, kill, relaunch, turn the Wi-Fi back on. The percentage must never decrease, and the send must end. Export the in-app log: it must carry `[UPLOAD-RESUME] session ... opened` and the `round N: ... KB/s` line, which is the phone's rate. Then cut the Wi-Fi once mid-upload: the next round must log `resumed: the server holds k/n part(s)` with k > 0. If the rate stays near 50 KB/s while the workstation reaches MB/s on the same Wi-Fi, it is the phone's path (radio or WKWebView), and that becomes its own entry.
