**The reading that closes it:** HARDWARE: one reading of the push on the Mi 9T and the iPhone 12 ([profiles-and-access](../profiles-and-access.md#d38b---a-personal-post-republished-with-its-authors-consent-decided-2026-10-19-as-built))

**Mi 9T half, READ 2026-11-15 on dev (`1.6.12-alpha.2`, build 100601202, the alpha account; app killed, the phone's push socket connected):** two associations of the beta web seat asked to relay one of alpha's personal posts (`POST /api/posts/<id>/republication-requests`, 201 each). The native service logged `showSimpleNotification: type=social ... deepLink=fr.emse.canari://republication-requests` twice (04:58:29, 04:58:31) and the shade grouped them: "<association> demande à relayer votre publication <post>" for each, in French. The tap opened the app on the PIN gate (cold) and, after the unlock, on the feed rather than on the request inbox. The two requests were withdrawn afterwards. **Open, not yet a verdict:** the tap landed on the feed, not the request inbox the deep link names (`fr.emse.canari://republication-requests`); whether a cold start after the PIN gate drops the destination (the fix `a push tapped after the session ended signs in to the page it named`, #3584, merged after this build) or the route is wrong is not measured, so the next sitting re-taps on a build carrying #3584 before this becomes a defect or is dropped. Build note: read on `v1.6.12-alpha.2` (built for dev, tag on `fa1bc2655`), not the stable APK; `v1.6.12` is the bump commit `0b6947d20` directly over it (version files and changelog fragments only), so the code read is the stable's.

**iPhone 12 half, READ 2026-11-15 on dev (TestFlight `1.6.12-alpha.2`, build `100601202`, app killed, app language English):** delta, holding `POST_AS_ASSO` in "Club Robotique Fictif", asked through `POST /api/posts/74c3f5a1/republication-requests`; the NSE ran (`type=social`, `outcome=server-content`) and the row read "Club Robotique Fictif asks to share your post" over the post's text "Banc reel 5".
