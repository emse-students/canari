## P2 - The in-app incoming ring is a media-volume beep (owner's first production call, 2026-10-30, not built; the ringback half shipped 2026-10-31)

The ring of a call arriving while the app is open is WebAudio tones at MEDIA volume and one 1.8 s vibration (`useNotifications.svelte.ts`, the in-app ring): silent with the media volume down, loud in silent mode, nothing like the phone's ringtone. Owed: a native ring through `CallBridge` - `RingtoneManager`'s default ringtone with `USAGE_NOTIFICATION_RINGTONE`, the ringer mode respected (silent / vibrate), a REPEATING vibration until answered, declined or ended. Kept out of the audio-UX pull request of 2026-10-30, which fixed the call itself ([calls](../frontend/modules/calls.md#a-call-behaves-like-a-phone-call-2026-10-30-after-the-owners-first-production-call)); the native ring's switches (`CALLS_NATIVE_RING_COMPILED`, Kotlin `CALLS_ENABLED`, `USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT`, `voip`) are a different surface and are not touched by it. The caller's ringback, the other half of this entry, is built: [the caller's ringback](../frontend/modules/calls.md#the-callers-ringback-2026-10-31). Delete with its fix.
