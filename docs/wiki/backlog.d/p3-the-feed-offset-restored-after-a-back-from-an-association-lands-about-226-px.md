## P3 - the feed offset restored after a Back from an association lands about 226 px lower than it was left (Mi 9T, `1.7.0-alpha.2`)

target: 1.7.0

**Found by:** the `1.7.0-alpha.2` reading of #3648 ([device-verification](../device-verification.md#170-alpha2)). On the Mi 9T, feed scrolled to 1500 css px, a profile opened from a post, an association opened from that profile, then the hardware Back twice: the app returned to the feed with the page scroll box at 1726 css px, so the posts sit about 226 px (a quarter of the screen) lower in the viewport than where the reader left them. The route is right; the offset is not exact. The iPhone 12 read the same restore as exact on the Organisations tab.

**Fixed in the next pre-release (fix, not yet read on a phone):** the entry records an anchor post and its distance from the top beside the offset, and the restore aligns the post, not the number, following the page until it is quiet ([section-navigation](../frontend/section-navigation.md)). The cause stays an unverified hypothesis; Pour toi and Organisations are both feed tabs, so both are covered, and the directory keeps the raw offset. The reading below stays owed on the Mi 9T, and the entry is deleted when it passes.

**What is not known:** whether the shift comes from the feed's placeholders being replaced by taller content after the restore (the restore waits for placeholders, #3648), or from autoplaying videos changing height. One run, one phone.

**The reading that closes it:** on the Mi 9T, repeat the route on the Pour toi tab and on Organisations, record the page scroll box's `scrollTop` before leaving and after the Back, three runs each; the fix owes a test that restores to the stored offset when the content above it changes height.

**Mi 9T, 2026-11-16 (`1.7.0-alpha.2`), the route the entry was meant to read:** a post published as an association, feed scrolled to 500 css px, a tap on the association name in a card header, then the header arrow and, in a second run, the hardware Back: both landed on `/posts` at 474 px, 26 px above where the feed was left (not 226). So the large drift belongs to the profile route (profile, then an association from it), and the chip route drifts by a smaller amount that two runs repeated exactly ([readings](../device-verification.md#the-mi-9t-readings-of-2026-11-16-the-owed-rows-170-alpha2)).

**Mi 9T, 2026-11-16 (`1.7.0-alpha.4`, carrying the anchor fix; [readings](../device-verification.md#the-mi-9t-readings-of-2026-11-16-on-170-alpha4)): `PARTIAL`, and a second defect.** Route: feed to 1500 css px, an author's profile, an association from it, the hardware Back twice. **Pour toi, 3 runs:** the same anchor post came back every time, but 45, 48 and 45 px lower in the viewport than where it was left (anchor top -513 before; -468, -465, -468 after; scroll box 1500 before, 1455, 1452, 1455 after), repeating to 3 px: the post is right, the place is not within the pixel the restore asserts. **Organisations, 3 runs: the Back returns to `/posts` on the "Pour toi" tab at the top (scrollTop 0)**: neither the tab nor the place is restored, 3 of 3. The entry stays open on both: the Pour toi 45 px (the restore ends on a proof that is satisfied while the anchor is still 45 px off, or measures the anchor in a layout that changes after it), and the Organisations tab (the feed tab is not part of what the entry records). Reading to close: the same route, Pour toi and Organisations, 3 runs each, anchor within 1 px and the tab kept.

**Mi 9T, 2026-11-16 (`1.7.0-alpha.4`), the Organisations Back read with a history logger (asked by the coordinator after #3672, now CLOSED: its theory is disproven, tab taps already push `/posts?feed=associations`).** The logger wrapped `pushState`, `replaceState`, `back` and `go` and logged `popstate` with `history.state`. Route, 2 runs: Organisations tab (the tab tap pushed `/posts?feed=associations`), scrolled to 1500 css px, the feed's "Plus d'actions" sheet opened and closed (`canariOverlay` states 10, 11 and 12 pushed), an association card link, its Membres page, then the hardware Back twice. **Result: NOT REPRODUCED.** Both Backs ended on `/posts?feed=associations` with the "Organisations" tab pressed, not on `/posts`; scroll box 1500 before, 1184 after (the box was already at 1179 once the first sheet opened, so the restore is within 5 px of where the feed stood then). In run 1 every sheet was closed by hardware Back, so no ghost was left and no `back` call was made by the app (0 `CALL back`). **Run 2 is the hypothesis's mechanism, seen working:** the sheet was closed by Escape, which left its `canariOverlay` entry in the stack (state still `canariOverlay:12` after the close), and then the second Back landed on that ghost entry (same URL, `canariOverlay` state); a `CALL back` followed at once, issued by the app, and the next `popstate` was the feed's own entry. So the extra `history.back()` of `settleArrival` exists and passes through a ghost correctly here; it is not what drops the tab. One unplanned observation: a hardware Back pressed on the Organisations feed itself (nothing to close) went to `/posts` on Pour toi, because the tab tap had pushed a second entry above the `/posts` the Feed link had pushed; the first sitting's route should be checked for such a stacked entry before the restore is blamed. Still open: why the 3-of-3 failure of the first alpha.4 sitting, whose route start (the entries already in the history) was not logged; the logger and the log shape above are the way to read it.
