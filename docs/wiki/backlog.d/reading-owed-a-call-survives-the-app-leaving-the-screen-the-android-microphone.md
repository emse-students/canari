## Reading owed - a call survives the app leaving the screen: the Android microphone foreground service

target: 1.7.0

**What is built:** a call survives the app leaving the screen: the Android microphone foreground service (WP-C-BG-A, #2162) and the iPhone's `audio` background mode (WP-C-BG-I, #2164), both in `v1.3.1` ([calls](../frontend/modules/calls.md#the-blockers-each-with-its-evidence), rows B8 and B9)

**The reading that closes it:** the twelve Mi 9T readings of [WP-C-BG-A](../frontend/modules/calls.md#wp-c-bg-a---the-android-ongoing-call-service-2026-10-20) and CALL-L1 row 12 on the iPhone 12

**Triage 2026-11-14 - MERGED with "a call that loses the audio focus holds its microphone and never hangs up"** (D056, decided 2026-11-12, `CallFocusHold.kt`, `CallService.onAudioFocus`, in `v1.6.12`; [calls](../frontend/modules/calls.md#a-call-behaves-like-a-phone-call-2026-10-30-after-the-owners-first-production-call)): during a Canari call with the iPhone, the Mi 9T loses the audio focus - the iPhone shows the microphone cut, the Mi 9T its banner, `focus`/`lost` then `gained` in the app log; once unmuted and once muted before the loss, the second comes back muted; the call is never ended. **Staging: the loss is made by another app taking the focus (a video started with `adb shell am start`), the same `onAudioFocus` path as a SIM ring; the bench needs no SIM.** **Left, both phones together, about 40 min on the Mi 9T side and 10 min on the iPhone side: the twelve WP-C-BG-A readings, the focus loss, and CALL-L1 row 12.**

**Mi 9T, 2026-11-15 (dev, `1.6.12-alpha.2`, build 100601202, the alpha account; beta in a web seat started with `callpeer.mjs --launch` - the earlier "never rang" attempt was a web seat without a fake microphone, not a product fault): the WP-C-BG-A readings and the audio-focus loss READ, five calls.** Evidence (`dumpsys`, `logcat`, the peer's per-second `getStats`) kept out of the repository.
- **(1) Locked 40 s and (2) HOME 40 s, READ.** The service stayed `isForeground=true types=0x80` (microphone) and the beta seat received a steady ~253 packets per 5 s, 0 lost, jitter 10-15 ms, through both gaps; no `closed code=1006` in the phone's log. (The peer's level read 0.000 only because the Mi 9T's room was silent: it proves the packets flow, not the voice.)
- **(3) The ongoing notification, READ.** Title "Canari Test Beta", "Appel audio", the peer's photo, a running chronometer ("Canari - 00:25"), the system "Raccrocher" and a mute action; the mute action toggled the call's microphone (the label showed "Reactiver le micro" after an in-app mute, and the app log `toggle-mute` then `update ... muted=false`); "Raccrocher" ended the call on both ends (`hang-up`, `left the foreground: hang-up pressed in the notification`, the beta seat's call ended).
- **(5) Speaker and earpiece, READ.** The call started on the earpiece (audio call); the toggle moved the active communication device to the speaker and back, `Actual mode = MODE_IN_COMMUNICATION`; the log says `mode already MODE_IN_COMMUNICATION (set by the page's capture) - left to its owner`: **the page's capture owns the mode, as the F1 review feared**.
- **(6) `dumpsys activity services`, READ.** `CallForegroundService isForeground=true types=0x00000080` during the call, gone after.
- **(7) After the hang-up, READ.** `Actual mode = MODE_NORMAL`, `Applied/Computed Preferred communication device: null` (the first dump taken 1 s after the hang-up still showed the speaker, cleared within 5 s), no notification left.
- **(8) A page reload mid-call, READ.** `CallBridge: a page loaded while the call service ran - the call of the previous page is gone`, then `left the foreground`: `pageLoaded` stopped the service, nothing stayed in the shade, mode back to `MODE_NORMAL`.
- **(9) The first-ever call through the microphone prompt, READ.** With `RECORD_AUDIO` revoked the accepted call raised "Autoriser Canari a enregistrer de l'audio ?"; answered "Lorsque vous utilisez l'appli", the service started at once (`start sent`, `in the foreground: microphone service`): neither refused nor missed.
- **(10) Swiped from Recents mid-call, READ.** The service stopped (`the page that held the call is gone`), the notification went and the beta seat's call ended.
- **(12) Notifications DENIED, READ.** With `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` revoked the service still held the call: `in the foreground: microphone service, wake lock held, notifications shown=false`, `isForeground=true types=0x80`, and no `canari_call_ongoing` notification record.
- **The audio-focus loss (D056), READ.** Another app taking the focus (the Photos app playing a video, started with `adb shell am start ... -p com.google.android.apps.photos`; the system's chooser refuses a bare `file://` URI) logged `audio focus LOSS_TRANSIENT ... the microphone is held` and an `update ... muted=true`; back on screen `audio focus back ... the member's mute state returns` and `update ... muted=false`; the call was never ended. Muted before the loss, the call came back MUTED (the notification read "Reactiver le micro", the in-app button "Activer le micro"). The peer kept receiving packets throughout.
- **(4) Back at the root during a call: FAILS ON THE SHIPPED BUILD, ALREADY FIXED ON `main`, so the reading is DELETED.** On `1.6.12-alpha.2` (and so `v1.6.12`) Back from the root tab FINISHED the activity (`MainActivity: onDestroy ... finishing=true`, the process died, the call ended on both ends). That is the defect the owner reported and #3544 fixed (`moveTaskToBack` at a root tab, in `main` since 2026-11-14, unreleased): not a new defect, and a reading of code the build does not carry is not a reading. It comes back with the first pre-release carrying #3544 as part of [the root-Back exit](../frontend/mobile.md#back-at-a-root-tab-goes-to-the-background-owner-2026-11-14).
- **(11) A Bluetooth and a wired headset mid-call: DELETED, no such hardware on the bench.** The Telecom log lists `supportedRouteMask: EARPIECE, SPEAKER` and no Bluetooth device; the Mi 9T bench has neither a headset nor a Bluetooth peripheral, and the routing code (`pickCallOutput`) is pinned by `OngoingCallTest`. It stays unread until a headset exists, and no longer blocks the entry.
