## P2 - a group the iPhone joined while the app was in front is not drawn in the chat list until the app is left and resumed

target: 1.7.0

**Read twice on 2026-11-16, iPhone 12, `1.7.0-alpha.5`, dev ([readings](../device-verification.md#the-iphone-12-readings-of-2026-11-16-sixth-sitting-170-alpha5-a-ten-minute-live-and-a-newcomer-after-a-nickname)):** `delta` (a web seat) created a group and added `gamma` through "Ajouter" while the phone sat in front on the chat list. Both times the phone joined within a second (`[QUEUE] Processing Welcome`, `[WELCOME] Group ... ready`, `[SYNC] Welcome processed for ..., refreshing...`, `[QUEUE] Drain complete`), and both times **the list did not draw the row**: the first stayed absent for 3 minutes through a tab change and a pull-down, the second for more than 80 s with the tree read every 10 s. After `button home` then `launch` (a resume) the first screen already listed it, with the right age and the preview "Canari Test Delta a ajoute Canari Test Gamma au groupe". Nothing in the log between "refreshing..." and the resume says why the list was not told.

**Unknown:** whether it is the list's refresh after a Welcome on WebKit, or the page not treating a driven foreground as visible; the web seat of the same account drew its row at once. **To do:** read the code path after `[SYNC] Welcome processed ..., refreshing...` (the entry [a group joined in the background before its row existed](reading-owed-a-group-joined-in-the-background-before-its-row-existed-the.md) is the neighbour: its fix writes and announces the row for the "already held" Welcome; this one is a plain Welcome), reproduce on the phone with the list in front, then fix where the cause is.
