## P3 - creating a group sometimes makes the creating device discover it, and once recover it

target: 1.7.0

**Seen on the web rig, 2026-11-16 (`dev.68983e3`), by the LEAF-REFRESH-1 runner, in 3 of its 5 runs (two group creations per run), and in none of the three runs recorded for it on `dev.9ca790c` (`1.7.0-alpha.2`)** - so look first at what `1.7.0-alpha.3` changed in the creation or discovery path. The owner's device (W6) created a group and wrote `[DISCOVERY] Placeholder <g>... created.` for it between `create_group` and `add_members_bulk`: a group it is creating was discovered as if another device had made it. In one run the sweep went on to recover it: 31 ms after `add_members_bulk` started and 0.2 s before the creation finished, `[READD] <g>... attempt starting`, `[PENDING] Group <g>... absent locally -> recovery requested`, then `[READD] <g>... already in WASM - skip (call forgetGroup before recovery if out of sync)`. Nothing was lost and the Add landed (`[COMMIT] ... admits 13 device(s) at epoch 1`), so the rig's row passed and its console gate did not.

**Why it is filed and not narrated** ([charter](../../principes.md), the noise rule of `CLAUDE.md`): `absent locally -> recovery requested` is the line `NO_LOCAL_STATE_NARRATION` forgives only on a device holding no MLS state, and its own comment calls it "a finding anywhere a device is supposed to already hold what it is being asked to serve". The creating device holds the group by construction, so the line means two paths overlapped: the pending-invitation sweep read the group's roster row (written by the creation) before the local group was registered, and started a recovery the creation made redundant. The recovery heals cleanly, which is the shape of a defect rather than of a design ("a race that heals cleanly is still a defect"). The placeholder is the same overlap seen one step earlier: the discovery path took a group the creation had not yet registered.

**What closes it:** name what makes the sweep and the creation overlap and delete the overlap (the sweep must not take a group whose creation is in flight on this device, read from the creation's own state, not from a delay), then a rig window of many creations in which the three lines never appear. The measurement: `bun archive/leaf.mjs --row LEAF-REFRESH-1` mints two groups per run; a run is `PASS` only when the owner's console has no `Placeholder` and no `absent locally` line for a group the row minted.
