## Live replays - a finished live is kept three days and shown as a CanaReel (owner, 2026-11-07, designed and every question answered)

Asked by the owner on 2026-11-07, every question answered the same day:

- **Retention**: a finished live is kept and watchable for **3 days**, then deleted.
- **Always kept**, and *"il doit apparaitre comme un Reel dans les feed et l'onglet CanaReel"*: the
  replay appears as a CanaReel in the feeds and in the CanaReels tab, to the audience that could
  see the live. A live cut by moderation or ended by an erasure never becomes one (lead decision).
- **Download**: the same rules as downloading a Reel today, for whoever may see the replay.
- **The live chat is not replayed**: video only, the chat stays ephemeral.
- **And a review of what a CanaReel is** (*"il faudrait qu'on revoie ce qui est considéré comme un
  CanaReel ou non (durée de rétention etc), et peut-être afficher le temps qu'il reste au CanaReel
  avant suppression. A voir."*): an audit of every kind of item the CanaReels tab shows, its
  retention and where that rule lives, then a proposed model and the owner's questions, four at a
  time.

What this reverses: today a live keeps only its newest 20 segments and is deleted at its end - "no
replay", L3 and Q10 in [lives](../services/lives.md#deletion-at-the-end-plus-a-sweep-q10-l3).

**Designed 2026-11-07: [live-replays](../frontend/modules/live-replays.md)**, the only copy - the
storage (~1.4 GB per broadcast hour, a cap per account recommended), the measured segment join (one
playable fMP4 while the inits are equal, not across a changed init), a VOD list over the stored
ciphertext rather than a reel file, the audience and the one deletion path, the three days as durable
state, the download, the chat deleted at the end, the privacy texts, the CanaReel audit and the
owner's eight questions (R1-R8), and the work packages WP-R0 to WP-R8.

**Answered by the owner the same day (2026-11-07)**, recorded with the lead's decisions L-R1 to L-R4
in [the decisions](../frontend/modules/live-replays.md#decisions-of-2026-11-07---the-owners-answers-and-the-leads):
2 h of replay per account, counted against the person who films; reactions and comments as on a
Reel; no "replay available" notification; a moderation deletion of a replay is not a cut; the 3 days
count from the end; the time left shown on replays AND on CanaReels in their last 7 days. **And R6,
revised by the owner**: a CanaReel expires after **90 days** (chat media's idle number, one number
everywhere, replacing the camera reels' 30), covering camera reels AND any posted video that is
vertical OR 90 s or shorter - *"verticale ou 90s max. Parce qu'il y a du post éphémère horizontal
aussi"*; photos, and videos both horizontal and longer than 90 s, stay. Forward only for posted
videos; existing camera reels extended to 90 days. The read-only measurement of 2026-11-07 (figures
on the page: the store 755 MB, 68 GB free of 108 GB shared, ~20 GB a year projected) answered the
volume concern. Owed next: the init reading on both phones (WP-R0), then WP-R1.
