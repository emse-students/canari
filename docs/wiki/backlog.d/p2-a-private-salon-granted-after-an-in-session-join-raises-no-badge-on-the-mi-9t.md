## P2 - a private salon granted to a member after their in-session join raises no unread badge on the Mi 9T

target: 1.7.0

Read on the Mi 9T (`1.7.0-alpha.5`, dev), 2026-11-16, during the in-session join reading ([entry](p1-owed-one-live-reading-of-the-in-session-join-fix-2026-10-08.md), [readings](../device-verification.md#the-mi-9t-readings-of-2026-11-16-on-170-alpha5)). `alpha` joined a community from an invitation card in a DM; `beta` (its admin) created a private salon, `salon-priv`, and granted `alpha` read access through the salon's access panel. The salon appeared in `alpha`'s rail with its lock, no reload, and the messages in it were readable on opening, **but a message posted in it raised no badge**: `salon-priv` showed none for its first message while `salon-pub`, written to a moment later, showed 1 and then 2 (and drew the OS banner). A second private salon, `salon-priv2`, was created, granted and written to twice; neither message raised a badge, still none a minute later, and both were in the salon when it was opened. After `salon-priv` had been opened once, a later message did raise "1 message non lu", so the loss is of the salons granted AFTER the join, before their first open.

Related and not the same: [the first message after an in-session join on the iPhone](p2-the-first-message-after-an-in-session-join-raised-no-badge-on-the-iphone.md), where only the first-posted public salon of two lost its badge, intermittently. Here it is 2 of 2 on a granted private salon, and every message until the first open. Cause not named; the read mark of the granted member (`channel_members."readMarks"`) against the time of the first message, as `unread-communities.mjs` reads it, is the closing step. Charter: principle 2 (the essential is never lost): a message the reader was never told about.

**The reading that closes it:** the same stage on the Mi 9T and the iPhone (a community, a private salon, the member granted after joining, two messages), the badge read after each, three runs.
