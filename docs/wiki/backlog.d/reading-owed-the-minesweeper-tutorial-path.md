## Reading owed - the Minesweeper tutorial path, Le Pic, the miner's lamp and the mode modal read well on a phone (2026-11-27)

target: 1.8.3

**Built and tested in happy-dom, never seen on a device** ([the path](../frontend/modules/minesweeper.md#the-tutorial-path-owner-2026-11-27), [the modal](../frontend/modules/minesweeper.md#the-game-mode-modal-owner-2026-11-27)). The owner validated the design on a canvas on 2026-11-27 and ruled out any streak, flame, medal or points (charter, principle 3). It replaces items 11 to 14 of [the earlier reading](reading-owed-the-minesweeper-hint.md) that concern the tutorial's list of lessons, the pattern catalogue's route and the bottom sheet; the others (levels, ranked, pages) stay with it.

**What to read, on the Mi 9T and the iPhone 12** (a build carrying this change):

1. **The path**: `/games/tutorial` shows two tabs ("Les bases", "Motifs") with their counts; the zigzag of diamond tiles fits a 360 px width without clipping or horizontal scroll, on both themes; Le Pic stands on the yellow tile and does not cover the tile or its name; a long catalogue (29 tiles) scrolls smoothly.
2. **Everything is open**: a tile far ahead of the current one opens its lesson; nothing is greyed out or locked.
3. **Le Pic reads**: the drawing is crisp at 56, 96 and 160 px, the kepi has no insignia, the lapel is open on one side only, and the moods (thinking, pointing, glad, worried) and the flag pose are told apart at the lesson's size.
4. **A lesson**: the bubble beside Le Pic holds the goal and each lamp step without overflowing on the longest pattern text; the mini-grid, its marks and the three-colour legend fit above the bottom bar on a 5x4 and on the tallest catalogue board; Recommencer, the lamp and Passer are comfortable thumb targets.
5. **The lamp**: the "Indice" button pulses until first used and then stops; with the system's reduced-motion setting it never pulses; its flame and glow read as lit while a hint is shown; the unranked game's hint button wears the same lamp, legible at its small size.
6. **Solved**: solving a lesson shows Le Pic with the flag, the family progress, "Motif suivant" and "Retour au parcours"; the done tile is green with a check on returning to the path; the progress survives closing the app.
7. **The mode modal**: at the very first game on a device it opens by itself, centered, with its two cards and the levels under Entraînement; "Jouer" starts the game in that mode; afterwards the bottom pastille opens the same modal and it never opens by itself again; it clears the notch and the home bar and closes on the scrim, the cross and the Android Back gesture without leaving the game; "Libre" appears nowhere.

**What closes it**: items 1, 2, 4, 5, 6 and 7 pass on both phones, and item 3 on at least one. A failure names the item.
