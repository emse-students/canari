## Reading owed - a DM thread keeps its end after a return to the app with the keyboard up: ten iPhone returns (2026-11-23)

target: 1.8.0

**Fixed in code ([chat](../frontend/modules/chat.md#the-keyboard-comes-back-with-the-app-2026-11-23)); what is owed is the reading that closes it: ten home-button returns on the iPhone 12, below.** The history the fix was read from follows.

Seen once on the iPhone 12 on `1.8.0-alpha.2` and not chased: after the app resumed from the iOS
Settings app with a DM open, the thread was NOT at its end (the last photo sat behind the composer).
It was a resume position, not the keyboard path of #3984, which passed 3 of 3 the same day
([readings](../device-verification.md#the-readings-of-2026-11-23-180-alpha2-the-resume-with-salons-the-share-shortcuts-the-keyboard-on-a-photo-dm-the-inactive-iphone)).

**Steps to reproduce.** On the iPhone 12, open a DM whose last messages are photos and wait for them
to load (the thread is at its end). Open iOS Settings (the app goes to the background), then return
to Canari. Look at the thread and whether the "Scroll to bottom" arrow shows.

**What to read.** Export the in-app log at once and look for `left the bottom with no finger on the
pane` (the pane's own put-back line) and for the resume's `[CHAT_VIEW]` line; the cause is the
resume's placement of the pane, or the thread's measure after the return, and nothing says which.
Repeat five times, with and without a keyboard up before leaving. A thread that is at its end every
time closes the entry; one that is not names the cause to fix.

**2026-11-23, `1.8.0-alpha.3` ([iPhone 12 sitting](../device-verification.md#the-readings-of-2026-11-23-180-alpha3-iphone-12-the-resume-from-settings-the-pushes-name-and-logo-the-banners-the-share-logos-the-ios-27-launch)): FAIL once in 13 resumes, entry kept.** Five resumes from Settings (three without a keyboard, two with it up) and a 25 s stay left the thread at its end every time, the keyboard ones logging the pane's put-back line. Then a HOME-BUTTON leave with the keyboard up and a return a few seconds later left the thread above its end, the "Scroll to bottom" arrow showing, and NO `left the bottom with no finger on the pane` line at that resume (the lifecycle lines were there); the state outlived a relaunch until a tap. Six more keyboard-up home-button cycles did not reproduce it. So the resume has a path on which the pane's put-back does not fire; the next step is to log the pane's geometry at the foreground edge (scrollTop, scrollHeight, the keyboard inset) so the next occurrence names the cause, then fix it. Not a regression of #3984 (the keyboard path passed again).

**2026-11-23, `1.8.0-alpha.4` ([iPhone 12 sitting](../device-verification.md#the-readings-of-2026-11-23-180-alpha4-iphone-12-the-bell-after-a-re-follow-the-dm-thread-the-communication-banners-the-focus-rows-the-voice-note-the-composer)): reproduced 4 of 10, so this is no longer a reading but a defect.** The exact gesture, on the DM of `gamma` with `delta` (60 rows, three screenshot photos and a voice note above its end): keyboard up at the end of the thread, Home button, 3 to 10 s away, relaunch. Ten cycles away for 3 to 10 s: **FAIL at 4 s, 4 s, 10 s and 3 s; PASS at 4, 3, 5, 8, 5 and 7 s**; the time away does not separate them (a 6 s cycle found no WebView in the tree 2.5 s after the relaunch and is not counted). **Geometry at the foreground edge, read from WebDriverAgent (the app logs none):** passing and failing returns alike have the web view at 390 x 543 pt (the keyboard-up size) and the composer footer at y 479 to 543; in a failing return the "Scroll to bottom of conversation" button is on screen at (330, 339) and the last photo ends at y 544 or 566, that is 65 to 87 pt under the composer, so the pane sits about one photo-and-a-half above its end. **What the in-app log says:** nothing at the resume. None of the 10 returns, passing or failing, has a `left the bottom with no finger on the pane` line or a `[CHAT_VIEW]` line; the resume lines (`App in foreground ... resume prepared in 219 to 878 ms`) are the same in both. The put-back line appears only at the keyboard RAISES I made between cycles (7 of them, always `scrollTop 6464 (last placed 7533), 1069 px from the end`, so the engine moves the pane by 1069 px at every raise and the page puts it back). **A plain keyboard rise with no leaving ended above the end once** (the arrow showing, steady, a second sample of the same state), in the one rise checked. **What this names:** the engine's keyboard move is put back on a raise, and a keyboard re-presented by a resume sometimes is not; the log carries no line that tells the two apart, so the next step is a line at the foreground edge (pane `scrollTop`, `scrollHeight`, `clientHeight`, `isNearBottom`) and one after the keyboard settles. Evidence is outside the repository, with the harness (`r180a4-iphone`).

**The fix (#4023), and the reading that closes the entry.** **The cause, by reading:** iOS re-presents the keyboard at the return and WebKit's did-show reveal scrolls the thread pane, but the WebView is the size it left with, so the page saw no keyboard change, no keyboard transition was moving, and the move was read as the reader leaving (the fall logged at DEBUG, which the exported log does not record). **The fix:** the shell's will-show starts a keyboard transition, which its paired did-show (or did-hide) rests, so the reveal is put back like a rise's; the pane logs its geometry (`[CHAT_VIEW] ... page hidden|visible`, `... keyboard transition #T starts|at rest`: `scrollTop`, `scrollHeight`, `clientHeight`, `fromEnd`, `pinned`, `isNearBottom`) and the fall line is INFO ([chat](../frontend/modules/chat.md#the-keyboard-comes-back-with-the-app-2026-11-23)). **The reading that closes it:** the same gesture ten times on the iPhone 12; every return at the end, and any return that logs `left the bottom` names its transition. A plain keyboard rise that ended above the end once (no leaving) is read in the same sitting: its `left the bottom` line, now INFO, says whether it was a second engine move in one transition.

**2026-11-24, `1.8.0-alpha.6` ([iPhone 12 sitting](../device-verification.md#the-readings-of-2026-11-24-180-alpha6-iphone-12-the-dm-thread-again-the-composer-the-ios-27-sdk-build-the-broadcasters-column-the-voice-note-the-sign-out-row)): FAIL, 5 of 11, entry stays, the defect moves to [its own entry](p2-a-dm-thread-is-left-above-its-end-by-a-keyboard-reveal-that-lands-after-the-transition-rests.md).** #4023 is on this build and the gesture is the same: five returns ended above the end (at 8, 4, 7, 4 and 5 s away), six at the end. The new INFO lines name the cause: on every failing return the keyboard transition is `at rest` 36 to 45 ms before the pane's 912 px reveal move, which is then read as `not a keyboard move of the engine, the flag falls`; on the passing returns the move lands while the transition is still moving and is put back. A plain keyboard rise that ended above the end (the second question of the reading) did not recur in the rises of this sitting.
