## P2 - forms: a free comment and an "Autre : précisez" on choice questions

target: 1.8.0

Owner decision 2026-11-21 (read-only production survey: 6 of 18 questions of one anonymous survey were a
"Commentaire" long text after a yes/no, answered 20-57% of the time; 1 choice question in 42 offered an "Autre").
Built: the per-question comment switch and the one "Autre" option, stored beside the answer, shown in the
responses table, searched, exported (own column for the comment). The "Autre" text is allowed, never required.
Read on dev with a real form (fill, edit, export) before the stable.
