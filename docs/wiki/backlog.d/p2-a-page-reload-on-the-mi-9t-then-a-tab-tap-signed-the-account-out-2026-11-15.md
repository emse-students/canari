## P2 - a page reload on the Mi 9T, then a tap on a bottom tab, signed the account out (2 of 2, found by the every-menu reading, 2026-11-15)

target: 1.7.0

Found reading the Android hardware back and a reload on a section on the Mi 9T (`1.7.0-alpha.1`, build 100700001, dev, the alpha account, unlocked, the vault path off). Both times: `location.reload()` of the WebView over CDP while on a section (`/forms`), the page came back on the same section, signed in and unlocked; about a minute later a tap on the "Discussions" bottom tab ended on `/login` and the phone's log read `[PushEnd] ending this device's push registration`, `endForSignOut: erasing the push secret`, `[MLS] ending the native MLS session (sign-out, state files kept)`: a SIGN-OUT, with the identity provider's sign-out page shown in the browser tab, not a transport blip. A cold relaunch (process killed, then relaunched, several times in the same sitting) never did it, nor did the navigation-mode switch that recreates the activity: only the reload. Signing in again with the rig's login worked and the device came back as the same device.

**Why it is filed and not waved off as the harness's doing:** the rig's own note says a CDP reload of the Tauri document is dirt the harness makes (the IPC callbacks of a response issued before the navigation land in the new document), but the app itself calls `window.location.reload()` on real paths (`appVersion.ts` after an update, the `MlsFatalErrorBanner` action, the `useChatSession` and `BaseMlsService` recoveries, `indexeddb.ts`), and a session that a reload can end is a real user's. Not read: the page's console at the moment of the tap (logcat carries no WebView console for the app; the next run attaches `Runtime.consoleAPICalled` before the reload and keeps it through the tap), and which request answered 401 first. Cause unknown; a candidate to rule out first is the refresh cookie rotating twice (the reloaded document and the previous one both refreshing), which only a console and network capture across the reload can confirm or refute.

**The reading that closes it:** reload the page on a section, wait a minute, tap two bottom tabs, with the console and the network captured: the account stays signed in, or the first 401 and who sent it are named.
