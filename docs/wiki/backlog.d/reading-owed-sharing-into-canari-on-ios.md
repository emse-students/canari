**To read the suggestion row (instrument built, branch fix/ios-share-extension-logs):** on the first alpha carrying it, `python -m pymobiledevice3 syslog live -m "[CanariShare]"` while picking a suggestion in the share sheet. The line to look for is `[CanariShare] share extension opened with N item(s), intent=..., send_message_intent=..., suggestion=...`: no line = the sheet never started the extension (an OS-side or Info.plist cause); `intent=false` = it started with no intent; `send_message_intent=true, suggestion=false` is followed by `a suggestion whose conversation identifier is not of the suggestion shape was ignored (identifier present: ..., N characters)`; `suggestion=true` with no banner means the later stages are at fault (the next `share handed over` / `share notification posted` lines say which).
