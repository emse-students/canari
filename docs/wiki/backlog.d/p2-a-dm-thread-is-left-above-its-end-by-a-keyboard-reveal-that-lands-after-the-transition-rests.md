## P2 - a DM thread is left above its end when the keyboard comes back with the app: #4023 fixed 6 of 11 returns, the reveal lands after the transition has rested (2026-11-24)

target: 1.8.1

**Seen on the iPhone 12 on `1.8.0-alpha.6`, which carries #4023** ([sitting](../device-verification.md#the-readings-of-2026-11-24-180-alpha6-iphone-12-the-dm-thread-again-the-composer-the-ios-27-sdk-build-the-broadcasters-column-the-voice-note-the-sign-out-row)). The reading entry that this one continued (a DM thread keeps its end after a return to the app with the keyboard up) was read 10 of 10 on `1.8.1-alpha.1` and closed; its history is in [chat](../frontend/modules/chat.md#the-keyboard-comes-back-with-the-app-2026-11-23). Fixed by `fix/ios-keyboard-reveal-and-inset` (below), unread on a phone.

**The gesture.** The DM of `gamma` with `delta` (60 rows, three photos and a voice note above its end), the keyboard up at the end of the thread, Home button, 3 to 10 s away, relaunch. Eleven counted returns: **the arrow "Scroll to bottom" showed, with the last photo 65 to 87 pt under the composer, at 8, 4, 7, 4 and 5 s; the thread was at its end at 4, 3, 10, 5, 3 and 6 s** (`alpha.4`, without the fix: 4 of 10). The time away does not separate them.

**What the in-app log now says (the INFO lines #4023 added).** The geometry is always the same: the web view is 390 x 543 pt, `clientHeight 496` with `keyboard open 301px`, and the engine's move sends the pane from `scrollTop 6394` to `5482`, 912 px from the end.

- A passing return: the shell's will-show starts a transition (`keyboard transition #T starts`), WebKit's reveal moves the pane while that transition is MOVING, and the page logs `left the bottom with no finger on the pane ... the engine's keyboard move, put back at the end (put-back N of this pane, keyboard transition #T)`, then `at rest` with `fromEnd 0 pinned=true`.
- A failing return, five times, transitions #14, #18, #26, #31 and #36: the transition starts and is `at rest` within 36 to 45 ms, and ONLY THEN the pane moves: `left the bottom with no finger on the pane: scrollTop 5482 (last placed 6394), 912 px from the end - not a keyboard move of the engine, the flag falls (keyboard transition #N at rest)`. Example, #26: `starts` 08:57:47.714, `at rest` 08:57:47.750 (`scrollTop 6394 fromEnd 0 pinned=true`), the fall 08:57:47.795. The put-back rule spends one transition per move, and here there is none moving to spend, so the move is read as the reader leaving.

**Hypothesis.** The will-show / did-show pair of the shell rests the transition on the did-show (or on a 36 to 45 ms frame), but on some returns WebKit's did-show reveal scrolls the pane a few tens of milliseconds AFTER that rest, so the "engine move during a transition" rule of #4023 never sees it. The reveal is unconditional on the OS side; what varies is how long after the did-show it lands. The `alpha.6` line says the cause is the ordering, not the geometry.

**To close it.** Either hold the transition open until the pane has had a frame to settle after the did-show (a measured number of frames, not a timer) or treat a move whose size equals the keyboard-reveal size (912 px against a 301 px keyboard in a 797 px view, the same value every time) landing within one frame of a rest as the engine's; then the ten-return reading above passes eleven of eleven and the reading entry is deleted.

**The cause, read in the syslog of the same sitting (2026-11-24).** The keyboard that comes back with the app shows with NO animation, so UIKit's did-show follows the will-show at once - while the shell's rise is still waiting for the page's layout and its screen-update proof, BEFORE the WebView's frame is set. The rest was told from that did-show. Return #26: the page `at rest` at 08:57:47.750, then `keyboard rise #54: the page's keyboard layout is on screen` at .768 and the frame set after it, then the pane's move at .795, then `keyboard settled` at .904. Return #31: the rest at .915, the rise's proof at .918, and the move before both, with the document scrolled 301 pt (`compensating a document scroll: scrollY=301`) - WebKit's own keyboard inset, measured against a frame still full height. Passing returns are the ones whose frame landed before the did-show's rest. So the hypothesis above is half right: the order is the cause, but the move is not only the did-show reveal, and no number of frames or size of move would name it.

**The fix (`fix/ios-keyboard-reveal-and-inset`): a rest is two facts, and WebKit's keyboard inset is gone.** (1) In the shell, a rest heard while a rise has not set its frame is OWED and told by the settle of that frame, after its screen-update proof (`CanariTellPageKeyboardAtRest`, `CanariKeyboardLayoutSettled`, logged `keyboard at rest before rise #N set its frame - told once that frame is on screen`). (2) In the page, a transition rests only once the shell has said so AND the hand-off target has been spent by the resize (`noteKeyboardFrameAwaited` / `noteKeyboardFrameLanded`, `keyboardTransition.ts`); a rest heard first is held and logged as a WARN, since the shell now owes it after the frame, and a newer keyboard change drops a held rest (its own rest is owed). (3) WebKit's own keyboard inset-and-scroll observers are removed from the WebView (`CanariDetachWebKitKeyboardInset`, [its entry](p2-the-iphone-page-reads-a-doubled-keyboard-inset-and-stops-painting.md)), which removes the document scroll of #31 and WebKit's reveal of the focused field with it - the move the put-back was built for may never come now, and the page reveals its own fields. No clock anywhere. Unit-proven (`keyboardTransition.test.ts`, `keyboardViewport.appReturn.test.ts`, `ChatArea.scrollAnchor.svelte.test.ts`, `iosKeyboardRiseHandoff.test.ts`); the native half is verified by compiling, which proves nothing about running.

**The reading that closes it, on the iPhone 12, on the first alpha that carries the fix.** The `alpha.6` gesture, eleven returns, 3 to 10 s away: eleven of eleven at the end of the thread (no "Scroll to bottom" arrow), and in the exported log no `not a keyboard move of the engine, the flag falls` line at any return. **The reading does not need an engine move to happen**: with WebKit's reveal detached the pane may never move, and then there is no `left the bottom` line at all - the verdict is the thread's end and the arrow, and a `put back at the end` line, if one comes, passes too. In the syslog, `WebKit's own keyboard inset detached ... (scroll view inset bottom 0.0)` once per launch (an `ERROR ... detached too late` line fails it), and on a return the page's `at rest` comes AFTER `keyboard rise #N: ... on screen` (often after `keyboard at rest before rise #N set its frame`); no `a rest heard before the keyboard's frame landed` WARN in the in-app log (it would name a broken native order). Then this entry is deleted (the reading entry it continued is closed).
