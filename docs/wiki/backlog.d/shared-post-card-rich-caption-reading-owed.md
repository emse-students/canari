## Reading owed: the shared post / CanaReel card's rich caption

target: 1.7.0

Read on both phones (Mi 9T and iPhone 12) on a pre-release: share a feed post whose caption has a line break, a `#hashtag`, bold and a link into a conversation. The card must show the line break, the styled hashtag, the emphasis, and clamp to four lines with an ellipsis; a tap anywhere opens the post. Same for a CanaReel. Substance: [sharing](../frontend/sharing.md#the-shared-post-bubble-owner-2026-11-09-comme-instagram).
