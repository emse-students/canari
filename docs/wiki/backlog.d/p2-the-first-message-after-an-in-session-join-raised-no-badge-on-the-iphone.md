## P2 - the first message posted in a salon right after an in-session join raised no unread badge on the iPhone

target: 1.7.0

Read on the iPhone 12 (TestFlight `1.6.12-alpha.2`, dev), 2026-11-15, during the in-session join reading ([entry](p1-owed-one-live-reading-of-the-in-session-join-fix-2026-10-08.md)). Gamma opened an invitation card from a DM, joined a community the device had never loaded, and the rail listed `général`, `salon-a` and `salon-b` at once, no reload. A second account then posted `post-join salon-a` and `post-join salon-b` within about two seconds: **`salon-b` showed a badge of 1 and `salon-a` showed none**, four seconds later and still at ten. A third message in `salon-a` made its badge read 1, not 2, although opening the room showed BOTH messages decrypted and in order; two more in `salon-b` made it read 3 of 3. So the loss is the badge of the first message of one salon, not its delivery or decryption.

**One trial, not yet repeated.** It resembles the window the fix closes (a salon registered after the message that names it, so the message is read as already seen), now on the salon listed first rather than on the unknown-channel path. The closing reading is the same join repeated three times on the phone with the badge of each salon read after each message, then the cause named: the read mark of a salon at the join (`channel_members."readMarks"`) against the time of its first post-join message, as `unread-communities.mjs` reads it ([chat](../frontend/modules/chat.md#a-community-joined-in-session-is-listed-whole-2026-10-08)). Charter: principle 2 (the essential is never lost): a message the reader was never told about.
