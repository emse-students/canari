## P2 - the first message posted in a salon right after an in-session join raised no unread badge on the iPhone

target: 1.7.0

Read on the iPhone 12 (TestFlight `1.6.12-alpha.2`, dev), 2026-11-15, during the in-session join reading ([entry](p1-owed-one-live-reading-of-the-in-session-join-fix-2026-10-08.md)). Gamma opened an invitation card from a DM, joined a community the device had never loaded, and the rail listed `général`, `salon-a` and `salon-b` at once, no reload. A second account then posted `post-join salon-a` and `post-join salon-b` within about two seconds: **`salon-b` showed a badge of 1 and `salon-a` showed none**, four seconds later and still at ten. A third message in `salon-a` made its badge read 1, not 2, although opening the room showed BOTH messages decrypted and in order; two more in `salon-b` made it read 3 of 3. So the loss is the badge of the first message of one salon, not its delivery or decryption.

**Repeated 2026-11-15 (sitting 5, same build, dev): 2 of 4 valid trials reproduce it, so with sitting 4 it is 3 of 5.** Each trial: a fresh community of two salons, its invitation card sent in a DM, joined in session from the card, then ONE message in each salon from the other account about two seconds apart, badges read from the accessibility tree after the OS banner had gone (twelve seconds; a trial where the banner was tapped is void and not counted). Salon-a posted first, then salon-b: **trial 1 and trial 2b, `salon-a` NO badge, `salon-b` 1** (both still so at the last read); trial 3, both 1. Salon-b first, then salon-a: trial 4, both 1. So the lost badge is the first-posted salon of the two in 2 of 3 trials in that order, and not seen in the one trial of the other order (too few to say the order matters). App logs: the phone log was captured over USB on the void trial only and names nothing; the cause is still unnamed, and the read-mark against first-message time comparison below is still the closing step. **Confirmed, intermittent, not deleted.**

**Before the repeats: one trial.** It resembles the window the fix closes (a salon registered after the message that names it, so the message is read as already seen), now on the salon listed first rather than on the unknown-channel path. The closing reading is the same join repeated three times on the phone with the badge of each salon read after each message, then the cause named: the read mark of a salon at the join (`channel_members."readMarks"`) against the time of its first post-join message, as `unread-communities.mjs` reads it ([chat](../frontend/modules/chat.md#a-community-joined-in-session-is-listed-whole-2026-10-08)).
