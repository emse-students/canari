Read on the iPhone 12, sitting S17 of 2026-11-20: opening the app by tapping the share notice showed a banner again for a message already delivered, a possible DOUBLE NOTIFICATION. Not yet reproduced. First reproduce it (deliver a message, leave it unread, open through the share notice, read the notification log and the notification centre), then decide whether the foreground presentation or the extension's delivery path posts it twice. If it does not reproduce in three tries, close the entry with the evidence.

**2026-11-22, [I1 sitting](../device-verification.md#the-iphone-12-readings-of-2026-11-22-173-alpha5-the-backup-domains-the-banners-the-reactions-the-replay-bar-the-outage-drill) (`1.7.3-alpha.5`, iPhone 12): REPRODUCED, and it is not the share notice.** `delta` sent a text with the app killed (the extension showed it, `[NSE_OUTCOME] decrypted`); a Safari share to Canari, then the share notice held and tapped in Notification Center, opened the app on the picker, and the message's row in Notification Center went from "5m ago" to "now" (the `[NOTIF] Inbound in <group> while the app is open on /posts showing the list - asking.` line at the boot, the frame arriving again from the pending fetch). **The control, a plain launch with no share at all, did the same** with a second message (row "2 minutes ago" 3 minutes after the send; the same `asking` line at 04:19:36, `NSE_OUTCOME ... decrypted=1` one second before). So the cause is the cold start's pending frame raising a notification for a message the extension already showed: the same defect as [the re-announce entry](p2-an-iphone-re-announces-what-its-extension-showed-after-a-cold-start.md), to be fixed and closed there.
