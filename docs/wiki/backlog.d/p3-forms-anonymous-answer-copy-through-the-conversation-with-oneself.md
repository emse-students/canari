## P3 - an anonymous form's answer, copied to its sender through the conversation with oneself

target: 1.10.0

An anonymous form's answer is shown back to nobody: the server keeps no link between the account and
the answer (principle 1), and the owner refused a device-only copy on 2026-11-21 ("Attendre la
discussion"): the copy is to travel to the member's own devices through the planned MLS conversation
with oneself, end-to-end encrypted. Until then the fill page says "Vous avez déjà répondu"
([forms](../frontend/modules/forms.md#a-respondent-sees-their-own-answer-owner-2026-11-21)). Blocked on
the conversation with oneself, which no entry plans yet (hence the last tier); when it ships, the submit posts the answer there and the fill page reads
it back. A guest on a public form has no account and no conversation: out of scope.
