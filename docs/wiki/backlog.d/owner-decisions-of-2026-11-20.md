## The owner's decisions of 2026-11-20

Recorded so no question answered here is asked again. Each line names where its detail lives.

1. **The workstation incident of 2026-11-18 is cleaned up**, and the agents' access to the host and to production is restored under the usual rules. The suspension and the credential rotation it owed are lifted by that confirmation.

4. **The precautionary rotation of the TURN analytics tokens was done before the stable.** Production got a new token, and the old one was deleted after production used the new one. Dev's token was rolled again, because its GitHub secret had been left stale by the previous roll ([MIGRATION](../../../infrastructure/MIGRATION.md)).
5. **Nothing local on iOS travels to another phone, nor back onto the same one.** A member's new iPhone, restored from a backup or Quick Start, carried Canari's sealed state without its `ThisDeviceOnly` key; the owner's decision is to stop that leak. Every launch excludes the data directory, WebKit's stores, the preferences and the App Group from backups. The cost is accepted: an encrypted backup restored onto the SAME iPhone brings back no local data and no at-rest marker either, and the phone enrols as a new device. The charter ([principes](../../principes.md)) decides it: principle 1, *vos messages sont a vous*, wants no second holder of a device's state, and it wins over principle 2 for a same-phone restore. Principle 2, *l'essentiel ne se perd pas*, is still served: the history comes back from the account's other devices and from other members, and from a `.canari` backup ([backup](../frontend/backup.md)). Mechanism, inventory and what stays unproven: [mobile](../frontend/mobile.md#nothing-local-travels-to-another-phone-2026-11-20).
