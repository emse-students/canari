Dev's token was rolled again, because its GitHub secret had been left stale by the previous roll ([MIGRATION](../../../infrastructure/MIGRATION.md)).
5. **A finished live is kept as a replay only if its organiser leaves the "Rediffusion" switch on.** The switch is shown when the live is launched and is ON by default. Once the live has started the organiser can only switch it OFF, during or after the live, and that deletes what was kept (segments and anything derived); it can never be switched back on, because nothing was kept. Viewers are not told whether the live will be replayed. The server is the authority. Charter principle 1 ([principes](../../principes.md)): the organiser's video is theirs to keep or to withdraw. Built ([the organiser's switch](../live-replays.md#the-organisers-rediffusion-switch-owner-2026-11-20)); the readings are [owed](live-replay-switch-readings-owed.md).
6. **The chat and the reactions sent during a live should replay in sync with the video, as on Twitch** (*"ce serait bien que les messages/réactions diffusés pendant le live puissent être présents à la rediffusion aussi, comme Twitch"*). A wish, not built: designed and planned with its open questions in [the entry](live-replay-chat-and-reactions-replayed.md); nothing is stored differently until the owner answers.
7. **The chat replay, four answers** ([entry](live-replay-chat-and-reactions-replayed.md)): (1) consent is a fixed line under the composer on EVERY live, "Vos messages peuvent être conservés avec la rediffusion", that does not say whether THIS live is replayed, and an author can delete their own line from the replay; (2) ONE switch: turning Rediffusion off also deletes the chat and the reactions; (3) reactions replay as anonymous sums per window; (4) planned for `1.7.3`, after `1.7.2`. Still open, with recommendations: the delay shift, who sees a line, hide, the bound, and the texts. Not built.
8. **Nothing local on iOS travels to another phone, nor back onto the same one** (confirmed by the owner, 2026-11-20). A member's new iPhone, restored from a backup or Quick Start, carried Canari's sealed state without its `ThisDeviceOnly` key; the owner's decision is to stop that leak. Every launch excludes the data directory, WebKit's stores, the preferences and the App Group from backups. The cost is accepted: an encrypted backup restored onto the SAME iPhone brings back no local data and no at-rest marker either, and the phone enrols as a new device. The charter ([principes](../../principes.md)) decides it: principle 1, *vos messages sont a vous*, wants no second holder of a device's state, and it wins over principle 2 for a same-phone restore. Principle 2, *l'essentiel ne se perd pas*, is still served: the history comes back from the account's other devices and from other members, and from a `.canari` backup ([backup](../frontend/backup.md)). Mechanism, inventory and what stays unproven: [mobile](../frontend/mobile.md#nothing-local-travels-to-another-phone-2026-11-20).
9. **The Lydia Responsable hand-over, four answers** ([payments](../frontend/modules/payments.md#handing-the-responsable-over-owner-2026-11-19)): (1) no acceptance step - the heir is told by a bell row, and that is enough; (2) a GLOBAL admin may remove a live Responsable from an association without a hand-over, after a confirmation TYPED under a sentence saying the number keeps its Lydia rights until it is removed - members and managers stay held by the departure rule; (3) an existing cashier, typically the Tresorier, is promoted in one step: the hand-over replaces their rights with the 13, with their own number, and closes their cashier row in the same transaction; (4) heirs are limited to members holding `MANAGE_PAYOUT_ACCOUNT`, so the heir can hand over in turn. Built behind the hand-over's switch, OFF until its [readings](reading-owed-the-lydia-responsable-hand-over-runs-against-homologation.md).
