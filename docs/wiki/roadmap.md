# Roadmap - how the plan is made, its dated commitments and its log

**The plan itself is [plan](plan.md)**: every open backlog entry under the version it is planned for,
generated from the `target:` line each entry carries (owner, 2026-11-13). This page keeps what a
generated list cannot: the hypotheses behind the order, the commitments that DO carry a calendar date
(compatibility removals), and the log of every replanning and milestone. The private companion (an
artifact the lead republishes) carries what must not live in a public repository: the open scanner
alerts one by one.

**How it stays true.** A new backlog entry names its version in the same pull request
([backlog.d README](backlog.d/README.md)). At every milestone - a merged pull request, a pre-release,
a stable, a reading taken on a phone - the lead edits the `target:` lines it touched, regenerates the
plan, appends a line to the log below and republishes the artifact. **When a version ships, the plan is
re-read whole**: the shipped version leaves `TIERS` in `tools/backlog-plan/plan.mjs`, every entry
still naming it is replanned, and the readings move to the new earliest version. This page is bundled
into the next real pull request, never a standalone one.

## Hypotheses

| What | Hypothesis |
| --- | --- |
| Versions, not dates | The plan names versions and no dates (owner, 2026-11-13: *"Les dates ne sont qu'une estimation, en vrai on fait le plus vite possible"*). A feature batch bumps the minor, a fix batch the patch; patches are not planned in advance, so `1.7.x` is not a target - a fix found after `1.7.0` ships in the next patch or targets `1.8.0`. |
| Pre-release cadence | One pre-release about every 2 days when main holds merged work and a green CI (main's CI takes 17-20 min, the release ~45 min with iOS and Android). |
| Stable cadence | At most one stable a week, only after the last alpha is read on both phones. Google Play: hours. App Store: +24 to 48 h of review. |
| Review and merge | Each agent PR is a draft reviewed before arming: 0.5 to 1 day. A plain migration merges after a pass on dev; a held one gets its approval line from the lead (delegation of 2026-11-03). |
| What the lead does not control | An owner decision, a hardware the bench does not hold, Apple's review. The entry says what it waits on; it keeps its version and is replanned when the version ships without it. |
| What `1.7.0` carries (owner, 2026-11-13) | Group calls (a mesh up to 4 in audio, 3 in video); "Envoyer a..." and CanaReels in chats, flipped in production after the 13 readings; screen share in lives on a desktop (the owner's reading); the slow-link chantier steps that are ready. And EVERY entry owed only a reading (*"au plus tot, marre de trainer ca"*), taken by the lead on the `1.6.12` and `1.7.0` pre-releases. |
| Order after `1.7.0` (owner, 2026-11-11) | The slow-link chantier first, then the backlog OLDEST first, then the phone-only readings. The remaining entries were spread over `1.8.0`, `1.9.0` and `1.10.0` in three equal shares by that order - every P1 first, then the oldest - with the MiConnect sign-in handoff (the owner's big chantier number 3 of 2026-11-09) and the live replays (decided 2026-11-09) pulled into `1.8.0`. |
| Updates | At every milestone (merged PR, pre-release, stable, phone reading) plus a daily CodeQL and Dependabot read. Replannings are logged at the bottom. |
